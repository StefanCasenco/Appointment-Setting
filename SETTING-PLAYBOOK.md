# Setting Playbook: Running a Natural Setting Call

How to qualify and book prospects on a trading offer without sounding scripted, and how to steer the call for every kind of prospect. Companion to the framework in `setting framework:script.pdf` and the 50 example calls in `roleplays/`.

---

## 1. The idea in one paragraph

The framework is a **checklist of buckets to fill, not a list of questions to read**. The prospect's first answer tells you what kind of person you're talking to. From there you follow what they say, go deep on whatever is loaded, and use short bridge sentences to land in the bucket you still need. By the end you must have every bucket filled, a clear gap, an honest read on money, and a locked time. The order changes from call to call, the destination doesn't.

---

## 2. Core principles

1. **Follow, don't read.** If they answer three nodes at once, skip them and confirm back. Never ask something they've already told you.
2. **Go in when it's loaded.** If someone drops something heavy (divorce, debt, a death, "my relationship is falling apart"), stop the framework and go into it right then. Returning to it three questions later feels fake, like asking "how's the gym?" after they said their dog died. Loaded answers are usually the real reason.
3. **Classify every answer in your head:**
   - **Vague** ("more money", "freedom", "be consistent") → dig for the real reason.
   - **Loaded** (60-hour weeks, a blown account, a relationship on the edge) → stay on it.
   - **Specific** ("I make $500, I want $7k") → confirm, quantify, move on.
4. **Dig twice, differently.** If the first "why?" gets nothing, try a different angle (what would change, what happens if nothing changes, tell me about the moment). If two tries fail, move to Current Situation and let their life answer it.
5. **Bridge, don't jump.** Every transition reacts to what they just said, then lands in the next bucket ("60 hours, Tuesday to Sunday... what does that do to your week?").
6. **Confirm back out loud.** Before the gap and after any long answer: "So basically your main goal is X, is that right?" It proves you listened and gets them to say yes to their own words.
7. **Let them say the problem.** The prospect should say the obstacle, not you. Ask, reflect, then summarise.
8. **Honest beats booked.** A no-fit lead sent to the closer wastes both people's time and burns trust. A kind, honest no keeps the door open.
9. **Never promise results.** No guarantees, no income claims, no "it only takes minutes a day". If asked, say what's true and send the detail to the closer.
10. **Never help anyone borrow.** Credit cards, loans, pension, emergency fund: all hard stops. Money for this must be money they can afford to risk.
11. **Know your role.** You're not the trader. You're the first conversation. Say so plainly when challenged.
12. **Silence is a tool.** After a loaded answer, wait. Count to four.
13. **Curiosity over persuasion.** The setter's job is to understand and decide, not to convince.

## 3. The spine

**INTENT → [middle nodes, order depends on the lead] → SHOW THE GAP → WANT TO CHANGE / URGENCY → FINANCIAL QUALIFICATION → PITCH → SCHEDULE & LOCK → COMMITMENT TO SHOW UP**

Intent is always first. Everything from Show the Gap to Commitment is always last and always in that order.

### The middle nodes

| Code | Node | What you're after | Typical questions |
|---|---|---|---|
| PE | Past Experience | What they've actually done, with numbers | How long have you traded? What does inconsistent mean? Are you green or red overall? |
| G | Goal | The real goal, in a number, and why | How much would it need to make for this to be worth your time? Quit the job or supplement? |
| EI | Early Impact | The cost of the status quo, emotionally | Why is that important now? What would that let you do that you can't now? |
| CS | Current Situation | Job, hours, income, how they feel about it | What do you do? How do you feel about it? How many hours? How long? |
| TL | Timeline | How long they've looked, and by when they want it | How long have you looked into trading? When do you want to be there? |
| WGW | What Got in the Way | The obstacle, in their words | What actually got in the way of starting? What do you feel stopped you? |

**EI is a jump node, not a fixed slot.** The moment pain surfaces, take it, wherever it appears.

**You can't show the gap until you have G, CS, TL and WGW.** If one is missing, ask for it next.

### The fixed tail

| Step | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Show the gap** | Reflect their current situation, desired situation, and the problem back to them | "You're in X, you want Y, and what's stopping you is Z. Correct?" Use *their* words. |
| **Want to change / urgency** | Test that they actually want it now | "You told me you want X. Are you actually committed to making that happen now?" Test low-urgency leads honestly. |
| **Financial qualification** | Confirm willingness and ability to invest | "Would you be ready to invest in yourself?" Then "how much have you set aside?" Honest about low or borrowed money. |
| **Pitch** | Connect them with the closer | Short. What the closer will do, not a sales pitch. |
| **Schedule & lock** | Two time options, then email, phone, accept now | "Tomorrow or [day after]?" "Morning or evening?" "[Time 1] or [Time 2]?" Have them accept the invite while you wait. |
| **Commitment to show up** | Prevent the no-show | "If something comes up, message me and we'll reschedule." Videos to watch, quiet place, a computer. |

## 4. Reading the first answer (the router)

What they say to the intent question tells you which row to follow.

| They say | Route | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "I'm new / never traded" | Beginner | Learn from scratch |
| "I've been watching videos" | Watcher | Check if anything's ever been live |
| "I started but can't figure it out" / "I can't do it myself" | Stuck | Past experience right away |
| "I want to be consistent" / "inconsistent" | Inconsistent | Past experience, then impact |
| "I want to make more money" | Vague | "Everyone does, why now?" → Goal |
| "I want freedom" | Vague | "What would that look like in your day-to-day?" → Goal |
| "I want to get out of my job" | Escape | Current situation right away |
| "I'm profitable, want to scale" | Pro | Numbers, short call |
| "I got burned before" | Burned | Past experience, then impact |
| "Just looking / curious" | Possible tire-kicker | Two digs, then decide |
| "How much is it?" | Price-first | Park it, keep going |

**Rule:** go to the node their answer points to, then fill the others.

| 8 | Pro scaler | PE → G → WGW → TL → CS | Numbers, what a mentor would add. Keep it short |
| 9 | Burned course buyer | PE → EI → WGW → G → TL → CS | What they bought, what was missing, trust |
| 10 | Blown-account survivor | PE → EI → CS → G → TL → WGW | Amount lost, state of mind, capital left |

|---|---|---|---|

| 16 | Supplementer | G → CS → TL → PE → WGW | How much extra, for what |
| 17 | Debt / financial pressure | EI → CS → TL → G → PE → WGW | Pressure level; **check capital early** |
| 18 | Family provider | G → EI → CS → TL → PE → WGW | What they're missing, who depends on them |
| 19 | Lifestyle / status | G → CS → EI → TL → PE → WGW | The real why under the flex |
| 20 | Location freedom | G → CS → EI → TL → PE → WGW | Cost of the current setup; confirm time zone |
| 21 | Business owner | CS → G → TL → PE → WGW | Hours available; why not the business |
| 22 | High earner, time-poor | CS → G → TL → PE → WGW | Realistic hours; reschedule risk |
| 23 | Capital holder | PE → G → TL → WGW → CS | Why active, risk tolerance |
| 24 | Career changer / 40+ | CS → EI → G → TL → PE → WGW | Age and tech worries |
| 25 | Young (18-22) | CS → G → TL → PE → WGW | Real capital; qualify early |
| 26 | Retiree | G → CS → TL → PE → WGW | Income vs purpose; keep pension out |
| 27 | Hobbyist | G → PE → WGW → TL → CS | Is there an income need? Test urgency |
| 28 | Prove-someone-wrong | EI → G → TL → PE → WGW → CS | Healthy motive vs revenge |

### Combining types

A call is usually one experience type plus one driver. Use the **experience order**, and put the driver's lead node first right after PE.

- *Stuck trader + escape artist:* PE → CS → EI → G → TL → WGW.
- *Beginner + family provider:* G → EI → CS → TL → WGW.
- If they turn out to be a different type mid-call, switch paths as soon as you hear it.

## 6. Attitude: how to steer

Attitude doesn't change the path, it changes how you handle the call.

| # | Type | Signal | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 29 | Skeptic | "Is this a scam?" | Agree with the instinct, don't defend. Say what you will and won't ask. Give checkable things, not "trust me" |
| 30 | Tire-kicker / downloader | "Just looking", "idk" | Two digs from different angles. Then a kind exit |
| 31 | Gambler / get-rich-quick | "$500 to $50k" | Don't mock the dream, test the timeline. Disqualify if they won't move |
| 32 | Passive seeker | "Do you give signals?" | "Do you want to learn it or have it done?" Disqualify if only the second |
| 33 | Price-first | "How much?" | Park it with a reason, then flip: "What number feels comfortable?" |
| 34 | Overtalker | Long tangents | Warm interruptions. Find the one real sentence and reflect it |
| 35 | Guarded / one-word | "Yeah", "idk" | Closed or A-or-B questions. Tolerate silence |
| 36 | Ego / know-it-all | "I know all this" | Ask for numbers. Never argue. Let the closer handle it |
| 37 | Analysis paralysis | "Comparing options" | Get their criteria list and pass it to the closer. Don't criticise others |
| 38 | Needs permission | "Ask my wife" | Invite the partner onto the call. Their worry is your obstacle |
| 39 | Emotionally heavy | Divorce, loss, grief | Stop the framework. Be human. Pitch only when the moment is right |
| 40 | Over-eager yes | "Sign me up!" | Slow down. Qualify money anyway. Highest no-show risk |
| 41 | Hostile / venting | Anger at gurus or you | Don't match the tone. Go into the pain. Never criticise the previous seller |
| 42 | Spy / troll / competitor | Questions about *you*, the process, the script | Redirect once, then exit. Give no internals |

## 7. Disqualification: who you filter out, and how

Always honest, never rude, always leave the door open.

| # | Type | Signal | What to say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 43 | No capital | Nothing set aside | "I don't think booking you is the right move yet. Build a cushion, come back." |
| 44 | Can't afford the offer | Far below the program | Check once for more, then a respectful no |
| 45 | Underage | Under 18 | Ask early. Be warm. Don't take contact details |
| 46 | Restricted region | Unsupported country | Say it's about the platforms. Don't endorse workarounds |
| 47 | No time | Can't commit the hours | Be honest about the hours. Rebook if it's temporary |
| 48 | Wants a guarantee | "Promise I'll make money" | "No one can guarantee that." Then ask what they can accept |
| 49 | Language barrier | Can't follow the coaching | Patient. Honest about the program's language |
| 50 | No-show / ghost | Missed the call | Curiosity, not guilt. Rebook with a firmer commitment. A second no-show is a no |

**Hard stops (never book):** borrowed money, pension or emergency fund, underage, restricted region, someone in crisis.

## 8. Curveball library

| Curveball | Response shape |
|---|---|
| "Is this a scam?" | Agree it's a fair worry. Say what the call is and isn't. Offer the closer for the detailed answers |
| "How much is it?" | "I wasn't trained on pricing, we have different programs, and quoting the wrong one would be a disservice." Then ask their budget |
| "Send me the info" | "Of course. So I send the right thing, can I ask one thing first?" Then ask for the obstacle |
| "I need to ask my wife/partner" | Don't push. Invite them to the call. Ask what the partner would worry about |
| "I'll think about it" | "Of course. What would you be thinking about?" It's usually money, a person, or doubt |
| "I'm busy right now" | Offer a shorter version, or lock a specific time. Never leave it open |
| "What are your results?" | Be honest: "I'm not the trader, I'm the first conversation. You'd judge [CLOSER], not me" |
| "I'm in another program" | Don't criticise it. Ask what's missing |
| "I already know this stuff" | Ask for numbers. Don't argue |
| "Can't I just trade my pension?" | No. Clear, kind, final |
| Emotional break | Don't fill the silence. One human sentence, then wait |
| They go silent | Count to four. Then an easier, more specific question |
| They ask a technical question | "That's [CLOSER]'s area." One line, park it |
| Time-zone confusion | Confirm out loud and check the invite shows their time |

## 9. Bridge bank

Short sentences that connect a node to the next.

| From → to | Example |
|---|---|
| Vague money → G | "Everyone wants more, I've never met someone who wants less. Why right now?" |
| Freedom → G | "Freedom means different things. What would that look like in your day-to-day?" |
| G → EI | "What would that let you do that you can't do right now?" |
| CS → EI | "What does that schedule do to your week, your relationships?" |
| EI → G | "Is that part of why this matters now?" |
| CS → TL | "How long have you been looking into trading?" |
| TL (long) → WGW | "That's [X months]. How come you haven't started?" |
| TL (short) → WGW | "Props for taking action fast. What do you feel might stop you?" |
| PE → WGW | "When you look at the losses, what's actually going wrong?" |
| WGW → gap | "So you're in X, you want Y, and the thing in the way is Z. Correct?" |
| Gap → urgency | "You told me you want X. Are you actually committed to it now?" |
| Urgency → FIN QUAL | "If there was a way to get you there, would you be ready to invest in yourself?" |
| FIN QUAL → pitch | "Here's what I can do. I'll connect you with [CLOSER]..." |

## 10. Gap statement and handoff

**Gap statement (use their words):**
> "So from what I understand, you're [current situation] and want to get to [desired situation], and the thing stopping you is [problem], correct?"

**Handoff notes to the closer (log after every booking):**

- Name, type (experience + driver + attitude), and the path used
- Real goal in their words, and the number
- The emotional why (the loaded thing)
- The obstacle in their words
- Budget stated, and any flags (low, tight, borrowed, spouse involved)
- Urgency level (high, medium, low) and why
- Objections raised (price, trust, partner, time)
- Time zone and booked slot
- Anything personal to reference (kids, job, a story)

---

## 11. Safety and ethics

- **No income claims, no guarantees.** Say what's true.
- **No borrowed money, no pension, no emergency funds.** Ever.
- **Honest about risk.** Trading can lose money. Don't dodge it.
- **Someone in crisis comes before the call.** If they mention hurting themselves, stop, be human, and point them to appropriate crisis resources for their region.
- **Respect "no".** One reframe, then let it go.
- **Don't criticise competitors or past sellers.** Ask what was missing.

---

## 12. Pre-call and post-call checklist

**Before:** read the form or CRM notes (resource downloaded, location, previous contact), check the time zone, decide your opener (YES or FORGOT).

**During:** keep nodes visible, tick them as filled, note exact words for the gap.

**After:** log the type, the gap, the budget, the flags, and the next step. Note anything you'd do differently.

---

## 13. How to practise

1. Read one roleplay in `roleplays/` and cover the lead's lines. Answer as yourself, then compare.
2. Pair up. One person plays the lead, with a hidden truth and one curveball. Run it, then switch.
3. Change one variable: the budget, the attitude, the objection. See what shifts.
4. Replay your real calls against section 5 and ask: "Did I follow the person, or the script?"
