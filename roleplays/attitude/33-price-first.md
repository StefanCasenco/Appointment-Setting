# 33 · Price-First

**Layers on:** Self-taught Stuck path (PE → G → TL → WGW → CS)
**Lead:** Andre, 33, mechanic. First sentence is "how much?" and he repeats it.
**Hidden truth:** He's been quoted high prices before and fears being cornered. He's actually interested.
**Curveballs:** the price question three times, in different forms.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Andre? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded the futures guide, right?

**Andre:** Yeah. Quick one, how much is the program?

> **CURVEBALL #1.** Don't answer, don't dodge awkwardly. Park with a reason.

**Ștefan:** Good question, I'll get to that. I wasn't trained on pricing, because we've got different programs depending on where people are, and I'd hate to quote you the wrong one. Can I ask a couple of things first so I can point you right? 10-15 minutes.

**Andre:** Alright.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Andre:** I've been trading a year, can't get consistent, I'm just trying to see if it's worth the money.

> **[NODE: INTENT → PE]** "Worth the money" = value question, not a price one. Go into the pain.

**Ștefan:** What's the inconsistency like? What's actually happening?

**Andre:** Up some weeks, down others. Ends roughly flat.

**Ștefan:** What would you want to be making if you got it right?

> **[NODE: G]**

**Andre:** 3k a month. Enough to stop doing overtime at the garage.

**Ștefan:** When do you want that by?

**Andre:** Six months to a year.

**Ștefan:** What's gotten in the way?

> **[NODE: WGW]**

**Andre:** Honestly, no one to look at my trades. But anyway, what's the ballpark? Like under 5k?

> **CURVEBALL #2.** He wants a bracket.

**Ștefan:** I get that you want a bracket, but even a rough number from me could mislead you, since programs differ in support and structure. What I can tell you is it's a real mentorship, not a $50 course. Is that okay to hold for a few minutes?

**Andre:** Okay, okay.

**Ștefan:** What do you do for work?

> **[NODE: CS]**

**Andre:** Mechanic, 50 hours, lots of overtime. I'd love to cut it.

**Ștefan:** So from what I understand, you're a mechanic doing a lot of overtime, you've traded for a year but can't get consistent, you'd like 3k a month in six to twelve months so you can drop the overtime, and what's in the way is nobody reviewing your trades, correct?

**Andre:** Yes.

**Ștefan:** If there was a way to fix that, would you be ready to invest in yourself?

**Andre:** Yeah, but I need to know the number first.

> **CURVEBALL #3.** Flip it: ask his number.

**Ștefan:** Fair. What's the number that would feel comfortable to you, so that I can tell [CLOSER] what you're working with?

**Andre:** I could do 3,000. Maybe 3,500 if I had to.

**Ștefan:** Good, that's helpful. [CLOSER] will walk you through the programs and the exact price, and you'll hear it from him with the full picture. Sounds good?

**Andre:** Alright.

**Ștefan:** Free tomorrow or Thursday?   **Andre:** Thursday after 5.

**Ștefan:** 5:30 or 6:30?   **Andre:** 5:30.

**Ștefan:** Locked in Thursday at 5:30. Best email?... Number?... Accept it now.

## Improv notes
- If he'd refused to continue without a number, give one honest line: "Our programs run from X to Y depending on support, [CLOSER] will pin it down." Only if your team allows ranges.
- Reversing the question ("what number feels comfortable?") gets you both his budget and his trust.
- Price-first leads are often qualified, but scared of being pressured.
