# 40 · Over-Eager Yes

**Layers on:** Total Beginner path
**Lead:** Zane, 23, warehouse temp. "Sign me up!" in the first two minutes.
**Hidden truth:** Excitement without capital or understanding. High no-show and refund risk.
**Curveballs:** instant yes, "how do I pay now?"

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Zane? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded the futures guide, right?

**Zane:** Yes! I'm so ready. How do I join?

> **CURVEBALL: instant yes.** Slow it down. It protects him and the close.

**Ștefan:** Love the energy! 10-15 minutes, I just want to understand where you're at so I connect you to the right person. Can we do that?

**Zane:** Yes, yes.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Zane:** I want to change my life. I'm tired of being broke.

> **[NODE: INTENT → G]**

**Ștefan:** I hear you. What would changing your life look like?

**Zane:** Money, freedom. Everything.

**Ștefan:** If we got specific, how much per month would feel like a change?

**Zane:** Like... 5k.

**Ștefan:** What do you do now?

> **[NODE: CS]**

**Zane:** Temp work at a warehouse. Not steady. Some weeks I get 20 hours.

**Ștefan:** How's the money situation?

**Zane:** Tight. But I'll find the money for this, I swear.

> A flag. "I'll find the money" ≠ money.

**Ștefan:** How long have you looked into trading?

> **[NODE: TL]**

**Zane:** A couple of weeks.

**Ștefan:** Props for taking action fast. Have you tried anything?

**Zane:** No. But I'm a fast learner.

**Ștefan:** What do you think would be the hardest part?

> **[NODE: WGW]**

**Zane:** Getting started. That's why I want help.

**Ștefan:** So from what I understand, you're doing temp warehouse work, money's tight, you want about 5k a month to change your life, you're new, and what's in the way is not knowing how to start, correct?

**Zane:** Yes. So how do I pay?

> **CURVEBALL: "how do I pay?"**

**Ștefan:** Hold on, Zane. I love that you want to go, but I don't want you jumping in on emotion. If there was a way to learn, how much do you actually have set aside, money that's not needed for rent and food?

**Zane:** ...Not much. Maybe 200. I'd borrow the rest.

**Ștefan:** I appreciate you being honest. I'm not going to push you to borrow money for this, that's a way to get hurt. I'd rather you build some savings first, keep learning free, and come back with a plan.

**Zane:** But I'm ready now.

**Ștefan:** I believe you want it. Being ready includes having money you can afford to risk. When that's in place, let's talk. Fair?

**Zane:** ...Okay. Yeah.

**Ștefan:** Take care, Zane.

## Improv notes
- If he'd had the capital, book him, but spend extra time on show-up commitment: eager leads cancel as fast as they commit.
- "I'll borrow it" is a hard stop. Never help someone borrow for this.
- Enthusiasm is not qualification. Always check FIN QUAL, even if they say yes in minute one.
