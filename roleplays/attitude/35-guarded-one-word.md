# 35 · Guarded / One-Word

**Layers on:** Total Beginner path (G → CS → EI → TL → WGW)
**Lead:** Isaiah, 28, security guard. Answers in a word or two.
**Hidden truth:** He's shy and afraid of sounding dumb, or doesn't talk to strangers. He does want it.
**Curveballs:** "yeah", "idk", "fine", and long silences.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Isaiah? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded the futures guide, right?

**Isaiah:** Yeah.

**Ștefan:** Cool, 10-15 minutes, just to understand where you're at and if I can help point you in the right direction. Cool?

**Isaiah:** Sure.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Isaiah:** Money.

> **Don't repeat the same "what" question.** Switch to a story or a "why now" question.

**Ștefan:** Sure, everyone wants more of it. Was there a moment that made you go "okay, I should look into this"?

**Isaiah:** ...My friend's doing it.

**Ștefan:** Nice. Is he making money? What's he told you about it?

**Isaiah:** Says it's good. He bought a car.

> A little more. He's warming up. Keep the questions easy and specific.

**Ștefan:** What do you do for work right now?

> **[NODE: CS]**

**Isaiah:** Security.

**Ștefan:** Night shift, day shift?

**Isaiah:** Nights.

**Ștefan:** That's a grind. How many hours?

**Isaiah:** 48, 50.

**Ștefan:** What's that like on the body and the rest of your life?

> **[NODE: EI]** A feelings question may stall him. Make it concrete.

**Isaiah:** Tired. Don't see people much.

**Ștefan:** Yeah, nights will do that. If you could change one thing about that setup, what would it be?

**Isaiah:** Not working nights.

> Goal found, in his words.

**Ștefan:** So making enough trading that you don't have to work nights anymore. What would you need to make for that?

> **[NODE: G]**

**Isaiah:** Like 3,000.

**Ștefan:** Okay. Have you started anything yet?

**Isaiah:** No.

**Ștefan:** What's kept you from starting?

> **[NODE: WGW]**

**Isaiah:** Don't know where to start.

**Ștefan:** That's the biggest one. So from what I understand, you work nights, 50 hours, you're tired and don't see people, you want about 3,000 a month so you can leave nights, and what's stopped you is you don't know where to start, correct?

**Isaiah:** Yeah.

**Ștefan:** And do you want to change that now?

**Isaiah:** Yeah. For sure.

**Ștefan:** If there was a way to learn it step by step, would you be ready to invest in yourself?

**Isaiah:** Maybe. How much?

**Ștefan:** Different programs, I wasn't trained on pricing. How much do you have set aside?

**Isaiah:** 1,500, maybe 2,000.

**Ștefan:** Okay. I'll connect you with [CLOSER]. Free tomorrow or Thursday?

**Isaiah:** Thursday.   **Ștefan:** Morning or afternoon?   **Isaiah:** Afternoon, 3.

**Ștefan:** Locked in Thursday at 3. Best email?... Number?... Accept it now.

## Improv notes
- Closed questions (yes/no, A or B) are easier for guarded people than "tell me about...".
- Silence is fine. Count to four before you fill it.
- If you still get nothing after three tries, offer a lower-pressure option: "Want me to send you something and we can talk later this week?"
