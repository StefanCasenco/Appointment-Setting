# 34 · Overtalker

**Layers on:** Inconsistent path (PE → EI → G → CS → TL → WGW)
**Lead:** Linda, 51, office admin. Talks in long loops, every answer is a story.
**Hidden truth:** She's lonely and likes the attention. Buried in the stories are real answers.
**Curveballs:** a five-minute tangent about her ex-husband, and a phone call from her daughter mid-sentence.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Linda? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded the futures guide, right?

**Linda:** Oh yes, I did, I was at my sister's and she was telling me about this thing... (continues for a minute)

> **Don't interrupt rudely.** Wait for a breath, then use one fact from her story as a bridge.

**Ștefan:** Love that. 10-15 minutes, to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. Sound good?

**Linda:** Of course, honey.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Linda:** I've been trading a little, on and off. It's inconsistent, which drives me crazy, and let me tell you, my ex-husband was the same way with money...

> **[NODE: INTENT → PE]** "Inconsistent" is your anchor. Ignore the ex-husband for now.

**Ștefan:** I hear you. When you say inconsistent, how long have you been trading, and what does that look like?

**Linda:** About two years. Some good months, then I give it back. It's the same pattern, I just can't seem to...

**Ștefan:** What does it do to you when you give it back?

> **[NODE: EI]**

**Linda:** I get so stressed. I don't sleep. It reminds me of when my husband left and I had to figure out the money myself, and let me tell you about that, it was 2018 and...

> **CURVEBALL: five-minute tangent. Emotional material.**

**Ștefan:** (after a pause) Linda, I'm sorry, that sounds like it was really hard. I want to make sure I understand, so is that part of why having steady income matters so much to you?

> Acknowledge and tie the tangent back with a question. This is the "real why".

**Linda:** Yes. Exactly. I never want to feel that helpless again.

**Ștefan:** What would steady income need to be, for you to feel secure?

> **[NODE: G]**

**Linda:** About 2,500 a month on top of my salary.

*(Her phone rings, her daughter.)*

> **CURVEBALL: interruption.**

**Linda:** Oh, it's my daughter, hold on...

**Ștefan:** Take your time. If it's easier I can call back in 10.

**Linda:** No no, I'll tell her I'll call back. Sorry, honey. Where were we?

**Ștefan:** Where were we, 2,500 a month so you feel secure. What do you do for work right now?

> **[NODE: CS]**

**Linda:** Office admin, 40 hours. It's steady but it's not enough.

**Ștefan:** When would you want to get there?   **Linda:** Within the year.

**Ștefan:** And what do you think has gotten in the way of getting consistent?

> **[NODE: TL → WGW]**

**Linda:** I think I don't have a plan, I follow what I see online...

**Ștefan:** So from what I understand, you work in admin, you've traded two years and it's inconsistent, it takes you back to a very stressful time, you want 2,500 a month on top of your salary within a year, and what's in the way is that you don't have one plan to follow, correct?

**Linda:** That's exactly it, honey.

**Ștefan:** If there was a way to build a plan with someone checking your work, would you be ready to invest in yourself?

**Linda:** Yes. I've got about 3,000.

**Ștefan:** Great. I'll connect you with [CLOSER]. Free tomorrow or Thursday?

**Linda:** Thursday morning.   **Ștefan:** 10 or 11?   **Linda:** 10.

**Ștefan:** Locked in Thursday at 10. Best email?... Number?... Accept it now.

## Improv notes
- Short, warm interruptions ("that's such a good point, can I ask...") beat cold ones.
- Overtalkers often hide the real WHY inside a tangent. Listen for one sentence you can reflect.
- Watch the clock. If you're past 15 minutes with half the nodes missing, say: "I want to respect your time, can I ask a few quick ones?"
