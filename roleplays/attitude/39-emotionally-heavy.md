# 39 · Emotionally Heavy

**Layers on:** any path. The emotion is the conversation.
**Lead:** Callum, 40, recently divorced, lost his job six weeks ago.
**Hidden truth:** He needs to be heard first. Trading is a lifeline he's grabbing at.
**Curveballs:** he brings up the divorce unprompted, then cries, then apologises.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Callum? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded the futures guide, right?

**Callum:** Yeah... it's been a rough few weeks, to be honest.

**Ștefan:** I'm sorry to hear that. I've got 10-15 minutes, I'd like to understand where you're at and see if I can help. Is that okay?

**Callum:** Yeah.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Callum:** I need to rebuild. My wife and I split a couple of months ago. Then I got laid off.

> **[NODE: INTENT → EI]** Stop the framework. This is the dog-died moment.

**Ștefan:** Man, that's a lot at once. How are you holding up?

**Callum:** (voice breaks) Not great. Sorry...

**Ștefan:** Don't apologise. Take your time.

> Wait. Don't fill the silence.

**Callum:** I just feel like everything fell apart and I need something to hold onto.

**Ștefan:** That makes sense. Before we talk about trading at all, are you okay? Do you have people around you?

> A real check. If he'd said "not really", slow right down.

**Callum:** My brother's been good. I'm okay, just... a lot.

**Ștefan:** I'm glad you've got him. Can I ask what you're hoping trading would give you?

> **[NODE: G]**

**Callum:** A way to control something. And money. I'm worried about rent.

**Ștefan:** How are you set for the next few months?

> **[NODE: CS]** Gentle, important.

**Callum:** I've got severance. Maybe five months of rent.

**Ștefan:** Okay. What are you doing for work, are you searching?

**Callum:** Applying. Nothing yet.

**Ștefan:** I'm going to be honest, because I'd want someone to be honest with me. Trading isn't a quick fix for rent, and I wouldn't want you putting severance on the line while you're in a tough spot.

> Protective, not salesy.

**Callum:** I know. I just feel stuck.

**Ștefan:** What I'd suggest: focus on stabilising your income first. If you still want to learn trading in a few months, from a steadier place, that's when it makes sense to talk again. Would that feel okay?

**Callum:** Yeah... that's fair. Thank you. I didn't expect this.

**Ștefan:** Take care of yourself, Callum. Reach out anytime, in a month or in six.

## Improv notes
- If he'd had stable income and savings, and the emotion was past, book him, with care.
- If he'd said anything about hurting himself, stop the call's purpose entirely. Be human, stay with him, and give appropriate crisis resources for his region.
- Never use someone's lowest moment as a pitch. If the story's moving, it doesn't mean it's the right time.
