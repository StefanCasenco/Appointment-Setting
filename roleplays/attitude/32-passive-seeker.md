# 32 · Passive Seeker

**Layers on:** Capital Holder / Total Beginner
**Lead:** Diane, 45, dental office manager. Wants income without learning to trade.
**Hidden truth:** She wants a "done for you" fix, not a skill. It's not what the mentorship offers.
**Curveballs:** "Do you give signals?" and "Can someone just trade it for me?"

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Diane? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded our futures guide, right?

**Diane:** Yes.

**Ștefan:** Cool, 10-15 minutes, to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. Sounds good?

**Diane:** Sure.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Diane:** I'd like to earn some passive income. I don't have time to study it.

> **[NODE: INTENT]** "Passive" + "no time to study" is the flag.

**Ștefan:** Makes sense. When you say passive, what would that look like for you?

**Diane:** Someone gives me signals I can follow. Or a bot, or copy trading. I just want it done.

> **CURVEBALL: "signals / copy trading."**

**Ștefan:** Got it. Can I ask what's made you lean toward that, rather than learning to do it yourself?

**Diane:** I work 50 hours. I'm tired when I get home. I don't want to become a trader, I just want the money.

> **[NODE: CS]**

**Ștefan:** I hear that. What's the money for? How much would you need?

> **[NODE: G]**

**Diane:** 1,500 extra a month. To help my daughter with college.

**Ștefan:** That's a strong reason. I want to be upfront though, because I don't want to waste your time. What we do is teach people to trade themselves. We don't give signals or manage money. Does that change things?

> Be clear and kind. This is the reframe moment.

**Diane:** Oh. Honestly I'm not sure I want to learn it myself.

**Ștefan:** I appreciate you being honest. Here's the question that would change my answer: if you could learn it in a few focused hours a week, and the mentor kept you on track, would that feel doable, or would you rather not do it at all?

**Diane:** ...I'd rather not do it at all. I don't have the energy.

**Ștefan:** That's a fair answer. In that case, I don't think we're the right fit, and I'd rather say so than book you into something you don't want. For the goal of helping your daughter, a financial advisor or a different income path may suit you better. If you ever want to learn the skill yourself, reach out.

**Diane:** Thank you for being honest.

**Ștefan:** Take care, Diane.

---

## Improv notes
- If she'd said "I'm willing to learn if it's guided", continue the path and book. The reframe worked.
- Never promise signals or managed accounts. Never suggest an advisor by name.
- Passive seekers are a polite no, not a hard one. Ask the reframe question once, then listen.
