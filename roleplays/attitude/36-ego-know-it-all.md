# 36 · Ego / Know-It-All

**Layers on:** Method Follower / Inconsistent path (PE → WGW → G → TL → CS)
**Lead:** Chad, 30, real-estate agent. Says he knows everything and just needs "a little push".
**Hidden truth:** His results are weak, but he can't say that out loud.
**Curveballs:** "I already know all this" and a challenge: "what are YOUR results?"

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Chad? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded the futures guide, right?

**Chad:** Yeah. I'll be honest, I know most of this already.

**Ștefan:** Good, that makes it easier. 10-15 minutes, to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. Cool?

**Chad:** Fine.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Chad:** I've been trading for three years. I know what I'm doing, I just need someone to sharpen the edges.

> **[NODE: INTENT → PE]** Ego answers sound big. Ask for numbers, gently.

**Ștefan:** Three years, nice. What's a typical month look like for you in numbers?

**Chad:** I do well. Some months are huge.

**Ștefan:** Roughly how huge? And what about the other months?

**Chad:** ...A couple of months I'd say 4k. Others I'm down a bit.

> Specifics shrink the ego without you saying a word.

**Ștefan:** And overall for the year, are you up or down?

**Chad:** Slightly down. But that's because of a few bad trades.

**Ștefan:** What do you think the main cause of those bad trades was?

> **[NODE: WGW]**

**Chad:** Overconfidence, probably. I sized up after wins.

**Ștefan:** That's an honest answer, not everybody says that.

> **CURVEBALL: "what are YOUR results?"**

**Chad:** Not to be rude, but what are your results? Why would I listen to you?

**Ștefan:** Not rude at all. I'm not the trader, I'm the one who figures out if it makes sense for you to talk to [CLOSER], who is. You'd judge his results, not mine. Fair?

**Chad:** Fair enough.

**Ștefan:** What would you want out of this, if it worked?

> **[NODE: G]**

**Chad:** 10k a month, consistently. I want to treat it as my main income.

**Ștefan:** When do you want that by?

**Chad:** A year.

**Ștefan:** And what do you do now?

> **[NODE: CS]**

**Chad:** Real estate. It's slow right now.

**Ștefan:** So from what I understand, you're an agent in a slow stretch, you've traded three years with some big months but you're slightly down overall because you size up after wins, you want 10k a month within a year, and what's in the way is the discipline to stay consistent, correct?

**Chad:** Yeah. I guess.

**Ștefan:** And are you ready to have someone look at your trading and tell you where it breaks?

**Chad:** If he's actually good, sure.

**Ștefan:** That's the right bar. If there was a way to fix that, would you be ready to invest in yourself?

**Chad:** Yeah.

**Ștefan:** Different programs, I wasn't trained on pricing. How much have you set aside?

**Chad:** About 5,000.

**Ștefan:** Great. [CLOSER] will review it with you. Free tomorrow or Friday?

**Chad:** Friday morning.   **Ștefan:** 10 or 11?   **Chad:** 11.

**Ștefan:** Locked in Friday at 11. Best email?... Number?... Accept it now.

## Improv notes
- Don't argue. Ask for numbers and let them speak.
- If he'd stayed vague about results, say "I'll leave the numbers for [CLOSER], he'll want to see them." The closer deals with the ego.
- Ego leads are at risk of a no-show if they feel talked down to. Keep it respectful.
