# 31 · Gambler / Get-Rich-Quick

**Layers on:** Early Starter path (PE → WGW → G → TL → CS)
**Lead:** Brandon, 24, restaurant server. Wants to turn $500 into $50k. Has lost on options and meme coins.
**Hidden truth:** He's chasing a jackpot, not an income. Without a reality check he'll blow up and blame everyone.
**Curveballs:** "How fast can I make 10k?" and a flash of impatience when you slow him down.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Brandon? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded the futures guide, right?

**Brandon:** Yep. Let's go, I'm ready.

**Ștefan:** Cool, 10-15 minutes, to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. Sounds good?

**Brandon:** Yeah.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Brandon:** I want to make big money fast. I've got like 500 and I want to flip it.

> **[NODE: INTENT → PE]**

**Ștefan:** Have you traded before?

**Brandon:** Options, some crypto. Hit a couple big wins, then lost it back.

**Ștefan:** What happened with the losses?

> **[NODE: WGW]**

**Brandon:** I went too big. But next time I'll be smarter.

**Ștefan:** What does "big money" look like to you? What's the number?

> **[NODE: G]**

**Brandon:** 10k in the first month. Then 50k.

> **CURVEBALL: unrealistic goal.** Don't laugh, don't lecture. Ask for the timeline and the reason.

**Ștefan:** That's ambitious. What would you do with 10k a month?

**Brandon:** Quit serving, buy a car, live my life.

**Ștefan:** And how long are you willing to give yourself to get there?

> **[NODE: TL]**

**Brandon:** A month, maybe two.

**Ștefan:** Can I be straight with you? Most people who do this properly take much longer than that, and starting with 500 makes it harder. If it took a year to get to steady income, would you still be in?

**Brandon:** A year? Nah, that's too slow.

> The reality check lands. Watch his reaction before moving.

**Ștefan:** I'd rather tell you now than let you find out the hard way. The mentors here teach slow, rules-based trading, not fast flips. If you're looking for fast, this probably isn't it.

**Brandon:** ...What if I just risk more?

**Ștefan:** That's exactly what blew up your last accounts, no? I wouldn't want to sell you something that sets you up for the same thing.

**Brandon:** Yeah, true. But I don't have patience.

**Ștefan:** That's honest. My suggestion: if a year-long process starts to sound okay to you, message me and we'll talk. Until then, I'd rather not book you.

**Brandon:** Alright.

**Ștefan:** Take care, brother.

## Improv notes
- If he'd shifted ("fine, a year, I'll be patient"), you can continue, but check WANT TO CHANGE and capital carefully.
- If he'd argued, don't debate: "Maybe I'm wrong. [CLOSER] might say differently, but I want you to hear the honest version first."
- Don't shame the goal, test the timeline. The gambler tells you who he is when time enters the conversation.
