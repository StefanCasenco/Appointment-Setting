# 42 · Spy / Troll / Competitor

**Layers on:** none. Signal: the questions are about *you*, not them.
**Lead:** "Alex", claims to be a beginner but asks about structure, pricing, closer names, and scripts.
**Hidden truth:** Likely another setter or competitor gathering information. Or a troll.
**Curveballs:** vague answers, targeted questions, and sudden interest in your process.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Alex? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded the futures guide, right?

**Alex:** Yeah, that was me.

**Ștefan:** Cool, 10-15 minutes, to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. Sounds good?

**Alex:** Sure.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Alex:** Just curious. How does this call usually work? Do you guys have a script?

> **Flag #1.** A real lead talks about themselves. This one asks about the process.

**Ștefan:** It's just a quick conversation, nothing fancy. What got you curious about trading specifically?

**Alex:** I'm not sure. How many calls do you do a day? And who's the closer?

> **Flag #2.** Don't give names, volumes, or structure.

**Ștefan:** I keep the details internal, but I'm happy to focus on you. What do you do for work?

**Alex:** I'm in marketing. How do you pick who gets booked? Is it based on money?

> **Flag #3.** Pattern: three questions about the system, none about himself.

**Ștefan:** Alex, I'll be straight with you. I've asked a couple of questions about you and I'm getting questions about our process instead. Is there something you want to know about trading, or are you curious about how we work?

> Direct, polite, no accusation.

**Alex:** (laughs) Just curious.

**Ștefan:** That's fine. I'd rather use my time with people who want to talk about their trading goals. If that changes, message me anytime. Take care.

**Alex:** Okay, thanks.

## Improv notes
- Don't accuse, don't reveal internals. A calm "I'll keep the process internal" works for both spies and curious people.
- If he'd then given real answers about himself, treat him like any other lead.
- Log the call and flag the number if a pattern repeats. Don't spend more than 3-4 minutes on it.
