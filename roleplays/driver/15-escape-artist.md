# 15 · Escape Artist

**Path:** INTENT → CS → EI → G → TL → PE → WGW → GAP → URGENCY → FIN QUAL → PITCH → LOCK → SHOW UP
**Lead:** Marcus, 31, warehouse picker. 55-60 hour weeks, Tuesday to Sunday. Beginner with a few months of YouTube.
**Hidden truth:** His relationship is cracking because of the schedule. He doesn't care about the money, he wants his time back.
**Curveballs:** a vague "freedom" answer, and an emotional beat he tries to wave off.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Marcus? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded our futures guide, right?

**Marcus:** Yeah, that's me.

**Ștefan:** Cool, 10-15 minutes, to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. Sounds good?

**Marcus:** Sure.

**Ștefan:** So tell me man, what had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Marcus:** I want to get out of my job. I can't do this forever.

> **[NODE: INTENT → CS]** Script says "GET OUT OF THE JOB" → current situation, right now.

**Ștefan:** I hear you. What do you do for a living?

**Marcus:** Warehouse. Picking orders.

**Ștefan:** How do you feel about it?

**Marcus:** Honestly? I hate it. It's repetitive, my back hurts, the managers are on you constantly.

**Ștefan:** How many hours are you working?

**Marcus:** Like 60 a week. Tuesday to Sunday.

**Ștefan:** Tuesday to Sunday, so Monday's your only day off?

**Marcus:** Yeah. And I spend it recovering.

> **[NODE: EI]** Straight into impact. The schedule is the hook.

**Ștefan:** What does that do to your week-to-week? Friends, relationships, all of it?

**Marcus:** (short laugh) Not good.

**Ștefan:** Why not?

> Don't accept "not good". Two words, "why not?", and then silence.

**Marcus:** My girlfriend... we barely see each other. She works normal hours. By the time I'm home she's asleep. It's... it's been rough.

**Ștefan:** I'm sorry man, that's a lot. Is that part of why you want out?

**Marcus:** Honestly it's the main reason. The money's whatever. I just want my life back.

> **CURVEBALL: he waves it off.**

**Marcus:** Anyway, it's fine, I just need to make more money.

**Ștefan:** I don't think it's just about money though, from what you said. Sure, everyone wants to make more, but why now? What's changed?

> Gently decline the deflection, he just gave you the real thing.

**Marcus:** ...She said she's tired of it. That if nothing changes by the end of the year, she's out.

**Ștefan:** That's heavy. So there's a clock on this.

**Marcus:** Yeah.

> **[NODE: URGENCY, early]** Note the clock. You'll bring it back at WANT TO CHANGE.

**Ștefan:** What would you need to make from trading for you to leave the warehouse?

> **[NODE: G]**

**Marcus:** Maybe 4-5k a month. I make about 2,800 now.

**Ștefan:** Okay. So the goal is to quit the job and trade, build a life where you actually have time. How soon do you need that?

**Marcus:** I'd love six months, but realistically a year.

> **[NODE: TL]**

**Ștefan:** Have you tried trading yet?

> **[NODE: PE]**

**Marcus:** I've watched videos for about four months. Tried a demo. Haven't put money in.

**Ștefan:** Four months, what's kept you from actually starting?

> **[NODE: WGW]**

**Marcus:** I'm scared I'll lose the little I have. And I don't know what's real. There's so much out there.

**Ștefan:** So from what I understand, you're working 60 hours at the warehouse, you barely see your girlfriend and it's put the relationship on the line, you want 4-5k a month so you can leave within a year, and what's stopped you is you're scared of losing money and don't know who to trust or where to start, correct?

**Marcus:** Yeah. That's it.

**Ștefan:** And you told me the clock's ticking, are you actually committed to making this happen now?

**Marcus:** Yes. I have to be.

**Ștefan:** Good. If there was a way to learn this properly with someone guiding you, would you be ready to invest in yourself if that's what it took?

**Marcus:** Yeah. I'd have to be careful though.

**Ștefan:** Totally fair. It's a real mentorship, different programs, I wasn't trained on pricing. How much do you have set aside that's yours to invest, not rent money?

**Marcus:** Around 2,000. I've been saving for this.

**Ștefan:** Okay. I'll connect you with [CLOSER], he'll go deeper, and if it's a fit he'll walk you through it. Sounds good?

**Marcus:** Yeah.

**Ștefan:** Free tomorrow or Monday?

**Marcus:** Monday's my day off.

**Ștefan:** Morning or afternoon?

**Marcus:** Afternoon, 2.

**Ștefan:** Locked in Monday at 2. Best email?... Number?... Accept it now.

**Marcus:** Done.

**Ștefan:** If something comes up, message me. I'll send a couple of videos, quiet place, computer.
