# 17 · Debt / Financial Pressure

**Path:** INTENT → EI → CS → TL → G → PE → WGW → GAP → URGENCY → FIN QUAL → (honest check) → PITCH or soft exit
**Lead:** Greg, 37, delivery driver. $18k of credit card debt, behind on a payment. Thinks trading is the way out.
**Hidden truth:** He'd fund the program with a credit card. He's desperate, which makes him dangerous to himself.
**Curveballs:** "I need money fast", and "I'll put it on a card".

> This one ends **without booking**. The point is practising the honest exit.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Greg? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded our futures guide, right?

**Greg:** Yeah. Listen, I need this to work.

**Ștefan:** Okay, I hear you. 10-15 minutes, to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. Sounds good?

**Greg:** Sure.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Greg:** I need to make money, fast. I've got debt piling up.

> **[NODE: INTENT → EI]** The pressure is the topic. Go right in.

**Ștefan:** I'm sorry, man, that's stressful. What's going on with the debt?

**Greg:** About 18k on cards. I'm behind on a couple of payments. Interest is killing me.

**Ștefan:** How long has it been like that?

**Greg:** Two years. It crept up. Car repairs, a hospital bill. I'm just trying to get ahead of it.

**Ștefan:** What's that doing to you day-to-day?

**Greg:** I can't sleep. I'm snapping at my kids. It's all I think about.

> Genuine pain. Do **not** use it to pitch.

**Ștefan:** That's a lot to carry. What do you do for work right now?

> **[NODE: CS]**

**Greg:** Delivery driver. About 50 hours. It barely covers the minimum payments.

**Ștefan:** How long are you hoping to fix this by?

> **[NODE: TL]**

**Greg:** Honestly yesterday. Six months would be great.

**Ștefan:** What do you want trading to do for you? What's the goal?

> **[NODE: G]**

**Greg:** Make enough to clear the cards. Like 3-4k a month.

**Ștefan:** Have you traded before?

> **[NODE: PE]**

**Greg:** A little. I put in 500 last year, lost most of it. But I've been learning.

> **CURVEBALL: "I need money fast."** The honest response is to slow him down, not speed up.

**Greg:** I just need to find something that works fast. I can't wait years.

**Ștefan:** Can I be straight with you, Greg? Trading isn't a fast fix, and anyone who tells you it is isn't being honest. Most people don't see steady income for months, sometimes longer. Does that change how you see it?

**Greg:** ...I mean, I know. But I don't have other options.

**Ștefan:** What's gotten in the way of making it work so far?

> **[NODE: WGW]**

**Greg:** I was rushing. I traded when I was stressed, trying to hit big. It didn't work.

**Ștefan:** So from what I understand, you're a driver with about 18k of debt that's putting real pressure on you and your family, you want to make 3-4k a month to clear it, you tried trading with 500 and lost most of it because you were rushing under stress, correct?

**Greg:** Yeah.

> **Decision point.** Gap shown. Next is FIN QUAL, which is where the honest check happens.

**Ștefan:** Okay. And I want to ask you something because I care about getting this right for you. If there was a mentorship that could help, how would you pay for it?

**Greg:** I could put it on a card. I've got a bit of room left on one.

> **CURVEBALL: card.** Stop here.

**Ștefan:** Greg, I'm going to be honest with you. I don't think that's the right move, and I wouldn't want you doing that. Putting trading education on a credit card when you're already carrying 18k of debt adds risk instead of removing it. And the money you use for trading has to be money you can afford to lose.

**Greg:** ...But then what do I do?

**Ștefan:** I'd say work on stabilising things first. Is there anything you can do on the debt side, like talking to a credit counselor or negotiating a payment plan? That isn't me selling you anything, it's what I'd tell a friend. When the debt's under control and you have money set aside that isn't needed for bills, trading starts to make sense.

**Greg:** I didn't expect you to say that.

**Ștefan:** I'd rather be honest with you than book a call that sets you back. If your situation changes down the road, reach out to me and we'll talk again. Fair?

**Greg:** Yeah. Thanks, man. I appreciate it.

**Ștefan:** Take care, brother. Good luck, and I mean that.

## Improv notes

- If he'd had real, separate savings (say 3,000 not needed for bills), you could continue to the pitch. Debt alone isn't a disqualifier, **borrowed money** is.
- If he'd gotten angry, stay calm: "I hear you. I just don't want to set you up to lose more."
- Log it as a soft DQ, not a hard no. A lead who feels respected may come back in a year.
