# 26 · Retiree / Semi-Retired

**Path:** INTENT → G → CS → TL → PE → WGW → GAP → URGENCY → FIN QUAL → PITCH → LOCK → SHOW UP
**Lead:** Walter, 63, retired electrician. Pension plus some savings. Wants income that grows his nest egg and keeps his mind busy.
**Hidden truth:** He's bored and has been burned by a "financial advisor" before. Slightly lonely.
**Curveballs:** he's chatty and goes on tangents, and he asks if he can bring his son.

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Walter? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded our futures guide, right?

**Walter:** Oh yes, yes I did. Nice to hear from you!

**Ștefan:** Cool, 10-15 minutes, to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. Sounds good?

**Walter:** Sounds good.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Walter:** I'm retired, and I've got some savings sitting there. I'd like it to work harder. And frankly I need something to do. I used to work with my hands all day, now I'm watching television.

> **[NODE: INTENT → G]**

**Ștefan:** That's honest. What would you want from trading, income-wise?

**Walter:** Nothing crazy. Another 1,500 a month on top of my pension would be lovely. And I want to grow the pot for the grandkids.

**Ștefan:** That's a nice goal. What would the extra income do for you?

**Walter:** Travel with my wife. We've always said we'd go to Italy. And help my daughter with the mortgage.

> Real WHY = family + travel. Great rapport material.

**Ștefan:** I love that. What's your day like now?

> **[NODE: CS]**

**Walter:** I wake up, have coffee, do a bit in the garden, then... not much. I've got hours to spare. I miss being productive.

**Ștefan:** How long have you been retired?

**Walter:** Three years. The first year was great, then it got boring.

*(Walter tells a long story about his old job site)*

> **CURVEBALL: tangent.** Enjoy it for a moment, then steer.

**Ștefan:** That sounds like a great run, Walter. So how soon would you like to get something like this going?

> **[NODE: TL]** Steer with warmth.

**Walter:** Oh, soon. Within the year, I'd say.

**Ștefan:** Have you traded before?

> **[NODE: PE]**

**Walter:** Not really. I had an advisor, but he lost a chunk of my money in 2008. I don't trust many people with it now.

**Ștefan:** I'm sorry to hear that. What would make you comfortable doing it yourself, with proper guidance?

> **[NODE: WGW]**

**Walter:** I'd need to understand it. I don't want to hand it over. If I'm doing it, I want to know why I'm doing it.

**Ștefan:** So from what I understand, you're retired with a pension and savings, you want another 1,500 a month for travel and helping your family, you've been bored and want something meaningful to do, you got burned by an advisor and want to understand what you're doing yourself, correct?

**Walter:** Exactly. You've got it.

**Ștefan:** And are you ready to give this a real go?

**Walter:** I think so. I've got the time.

**Ștefan:** If there was a way to learn this properly, would you be ready to invest in yourself?

**Walter:** I'd consider it. I'm careful with my money.

**Ștefan:** I respect that. It's a real mentorship, different programs, I wasn't trained on pricing. How much would you be comfortable putting toward education, keeping your pension out of it?

**Walter:** I could put up to 5,000. Not touching the pension.

**Ștefan:** Good call. I'll connect you with [CLOSER]. Sounds good?

**Walter:** Can my son sit in? He's good with this stuff.

> **CURVEBALL: bring his son.**

**Ștefan:** Absolutely, the more the better. Just make sure he's on the invite. Free tomorrow or Thursday?

**Walter:** Thursday morning is fine.

**Ștefan:** 10 or 11?

**Walter:** 10.

**Ștefan:** Locked in Thursday at 10. Best email for the invite? I'll also need your son's, if you'd like him included.

**Walter:** Sure, let me find it.

**Ștefan:** Number for a reminder text? ... Accept it now, I'll wait.

**Walter:** Done, I think. Sorry, I'm not great with this.

**Ștefan:** No problem. I'll walk you through it. I'll send a quick guide on joining, and I'll text you before.
