# 28 · Prove-Someone-Wrong

**Path:** INTENT → EI → G → TL → PE → WGW → CS → GAP → URGENCY → FIN QUAL → PITCH → LOCK → SHOW UP
**Lead:** Mike, 35, plumber. His brother-in-law told him trading is "for suckers" and he'd never make it. He wants to prove him wrong.
**Hidden truth:** The need for validation is strong. It can fuel him, or it can make him reckless.
**Curveballs:** anger about the brother-in-law, and a "sign me up right now" over-eager moment.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Mike? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded our futures guide, right?

**Mike:** Yep. And I'm gonna make this work.

**Ștefan:** I like the energy. 10-15 minutes, to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. Sounds good?

**Mike:** Let's go.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Mike:** My brother-in-law thinks I can't do it. Told me it's for suckers and I'd lose everything. I want to prove him wrong.

> **[NODE: INTENT → EI]** The emotion is the topic. Go in.

**Ștefan:** That sounds like it got under your skin. What happened?

**Mike:** We were at a family dinner. I mentioned I was thinking about trading, and he laughed. Said I'm a plumber, not a finance guy. Everyone just sat there.

**Ștefan:** That's a rough thing to have said in front of the family. How did that feel?

**Mike:** Honestly? Humiliating. And then angry.

> **CURVEBALL: anger.** Don't feed it. Acknowledge, then turn to the future.

**Ștefan:** I'd be angry too. Let me ask you this, though: if you set the brother-in-law aside, what do *you* want out of this?

> Pull the motivation off the other guy and onto Mike himself.

**Mike:** ...Fair question. I want a better life. I'm a plumber, I'm good at it, but I'm on call, my knees are shot, and I don't want to be crawling under sinks at 55.

**Ștefan:** That's a real reason. How much would you want to make from trading?

> **[NODE: G]**

**Mike:** I'd like 4,000 a month. Enough to take on less work.

**Ștefan:** When would you want that by?

> **[NODE: TL]**

**Mike:** A year or so. Eighteen months at most.

**Ștefan:** Have you traded before?

> **[NODE: PE]**

**Mike:** Never. I've watched a lot of videos since the dinner though.

**Ștefan:** What's kept you from starting for real?

> **[NODE: WGW]**

**Mike:** I don't know where to start. And I'll be honest, I'm scared of proving him right if I lose money.

**Ștefan:** That's an honest thing to say. So the fear is losing, and then he's right.

**Mike:** Exactly.

**Ștefan:** What's your day like otherwise?

> **[NODE: CS]**

**Mike:** I'm a plumber, self-employed. 50 hours, and I'm on call on weekends. Income's up and down but decent.

**Ștefan:** So from what I understand, you're a self-employed plumber, you were put down by your brother-in-law and want to prove you can do it, but really you want a better life with less physical work at 4,000 a month within 18 months, you've never traded, and what's in the way is not knowing where to start and the fear of failing in front of him, correct?

**Mike:** Yeah. That's the whole thing.

**Ștefan:** And let me ask you this, if the brother-in-law had never said a word, would you still want this?

> **Healthy-motivation check.**

**Mike:** (pause) ...Yeah. I think so. He just lit the fuse. I've been tired of my knees for years.

**Ștefan:** Good, because that's a stronger reason than proving someone wrong. Are you ready to commit to doing this properly?

> **CURVEBALL: over-eager.**

**Mike:** Absolutely. Sign me up right now, whatever it costs. I'm ready.

**Ștefan:** I love the drive, but let's not rush. I'd want [CLOSER] to look at your situation first, and it's not something I want you jumping into on emotion. If there was a way to learn this properly, would you be ready to invest in yourself, within reason?

> Slow the eager ones down. It protects the lead and the close.

**Mike:** Yeah, within reason.

**Ștefan:** It's a real mentorship, different programs, I wasn't trained on pricing. How much have you set aside?

**Mike:** About 4,000.

**Ștefan:** Good. I'll connect you with [CLOSER], he'll go deeper, and he'll tell you honestly if it's a fit. Sounds good?

**Mike:** Yeah.

**Ștefan:** Free tomorrow or Wednesday?

**Mike:** Wednesday evening.

**Ștefan:** 7 or 8?

**Mike:** 7:30.

**Ștefan:** Locked in Wednesday at 7:30. Best email?... Number?... Accept it now.

**Mike:** Done.

**Ștefan:** If something comes up, message me. I'll send a couple of videos, quiet place, computer.

## Improv notes

- If he'd stayed angry and nothing else came up, the motivation is fragile. Ask what he'd do if he lost 500. A pure revenge motive makes for reckless trading.
- If he'd said "I'll show him my first payout", mildly redirect: "Let's focus on what *you* want to build."
- Over-eager yeses are the biggest no-show and refund risk. Slow them down, always.
