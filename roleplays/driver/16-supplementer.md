# 16 · Supplementer

**Path:** INTENT → G → CS → TL → PE → WGW → GAP → URGENCY → FIN QUAL → PITCH → LOCK → SHOW UP

**Hidden truth:** She's cautious, risk-averse, and fears trading means "gambling". She needs to hear it can be structured.

**Ștefan:** How much extra would make a real difference to that?

**Aisha:** Probably 1,000, 1,500 a month. That would change things.

**Ștefan:** What does it change?

> **[NODE: G + EI]** Real why = security. Keep that word.

**Ștefan:** I hear that. What do you do for a living?

> **[NODE: CS]**

**Ștefan:** Okay. How do you feel about the job?

**Ștefan:** Makes sense. How long have you been looking into trading?

> **[NODE: TL]**

**Ștefan:** Have you started anything yet?

> **[NODE: PE]**

**Ștefan:** What's kept you from taking the real step?

> **[NODE: WGW]**

> **CURVEBALL: safety.**

**Ștefan:** So from what I understand, you're a nurse working long shifts, you want about 1,000-1,500 a month extra to pay the mortgage down and have peace of mind, you've been interested for a few months but haven't started because you're afraid of losing money and don't want to gamble, correct?
