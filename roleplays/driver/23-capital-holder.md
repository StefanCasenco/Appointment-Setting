# 23 · Capital Holder

**Path:** INTENT → PE → G → TL → WGW → CS → GAP → URGENCY → FIN QUAL → PITCH → LOCK → SHOW UP
**Lead:** Frank, 52, sold a small business 18 months ago. Has a six-figure sum sitting in savings and index funds. Wondering about active trading.
**Hidden truth:** He's bored, not desperate. He wants a challenge, but he's wary of "gurus".
**Curveballs:** he wants to put a *large* amount in right away, and he treats you as junior.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Frank? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded our futures guide, right?

**Frank:** I did. Frankly I'm not sure why.

**Ștefan:** Fair enough. 10-15 minutes, to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. If it's not for you, I'll say so. Good?

**Frank:** Go on.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Frank:** I've got capital and I want to see whether actively trading it makes sense, instead of leaving it in index funds.

> **[NODE: INTENT → PE]** Capital + curiosity. Ask what he's done.

**Ștefan:** Makes sense. Have you traded actively before?

**Frank:** Some options years ago. Mostly buy and hold. Nothing serious.

**Ștefan:** What do you want to get from active trading, specifically?

> **[NODE: G]**

**Frank:** A better return than the index. But honestly, also something to do. I sold my company a year and a half ago and I miss the challenge.

**Ștefan:** So income, but also purpose?

**Frank:** Both. Probably more purpose than income if I'm honest.

**Ștefan:** How much would you want it to make, so it feels worth it?

**Frank:** I don't need it to pay bills. Something like 3-5k a month would feel like a win.

**Ștefan:** When would you want to see that?

> **[NODE: TL]**

**Frank:** I'm in no hurry. A couple of years is fine, if it's real.

**Ștefan:** What's kept you from starting properly?

> **[NODE: WGW]**

**Frank:** I don't trust most of what I see online. I'd want to learn from someone who actually trades, not a salesman.

> **CURVEBALL: he treats you as junior.**

**Frank:** No offence, but you sound young. What's your experience?

**Ștefan:** None taken. I'm not the trader, I'm the first conversation. I talk to people to see if it makes sense for them to speak to [CLOSER], who is a trader. You'd judge him, not me. Fair?

> Own your role. Don't pretend to be a trader.

**Frank:** That's fair.

**Ștefan:** What's your day to day like now?

> **[NODE: CS]**

**Frank:** Semi-retired. Some consulting, a few hours a week. Plenty of time.

**Ștefan:** So from what I understand, you've got capital and time, you want to see if active trading beats index funds and gives you a challenge, you'd like around 3-5k a month in a couple of years, and what's stopped you is you don't trust most of what's out there and want to learn from a real trader, correct?

**Frank:** Yes. Well summarised.

**Ștefan:** And are you ready to give it real effort?

**Frank:** I wouldn't be on this call if I wasn't curious.

**Ștefan:** If there was a way to learn properly from a real trader, would you be ready to invest in yourself?

**Frank:** If it's genuine, yes.

> **CURVEBALL: large capital.**

**Frank:** I was thinking of putting 100k into a trading account to start.

**Ștefan:** That's a lot, and I'd be careful. That's a conversation for [CLOSER], who can talk about sizing and risk. A mentor will probably tell you to start much smaller while you learn. Does that sound okay?

> Don't encourage big sizing. It's a risk flag, and it's not your call.

**Frank:** Honestly that's reassuring. Good.

**Ștefan:** It's a real mentorship, different programs, I wasn't trained on pricing. How much would you set aside for education?

**Frank:** Up to 10k.

**Ștefan:** Great. I'll connect you with [CLOSER]. Sounds good?

**Frank:** Yes.

**Ștefan:** Free tomorrow or Friday?

**Frank:** Friday morning.

**Ștefan:** 10 or 11?

**Frank:** 10.

**Ștefan:** Locked in Friday at 10. Best email?... Number?... Accept it now.

**Frank:** Done.

**Ștefan:** I'll send a couple of videos, quiet place, computer.
