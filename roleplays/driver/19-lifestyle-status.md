# 19 · Lifestyle / Status

**Path:** INTENT → G → CS → EI → TL → PE → WGW → GAP → URGENCY → FIN QUAL → PITCH → LOCK → SHOW UP
**Lead:** Tyler, 25, sales rep. Follows trading influencers, wants the cars and the travel.
**Hidden truth:** Under the flex, he feels behind his friends and wants to prove himself. The lifestyle talk is a cover for insecurity.
**Curveballs:** over-the-top goals ("a million a year"), and he name-drops influencers.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Tyler? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded our futures guide, right?

**Tyler:** Yeah bro, that's me.

**Ștefan:** Cool, 10-15 minutes, to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. Sounds good?

**Tyler:** Let's go.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Tyler:** The lifestyle, bro. I wanna be able to work from anywhere, travel, nice car. Do my own thing.

> **[NODE: INTENT → G]** Script: "FREEDOM" → ask what it looks like, day to day.

**Ștefan:** Sure, I hear you. When you say that lifestyle, what does it actually look like for you in a normal week?

**Tyler:** Waking up whenever. Trading for a couple hours. Gym, beach, whatever. Not answering to anyone.

**Ștefan:** And how much do you need to be making for that to be real?

**Tyler:** I'm talking a million a year, bro. That's the goal.

> **CURVEBALL: inflated goal.** Don't laugh, don't debate. Ask what's under it.

**Ștefan:** That's a big number. If it took you to, say, 10k a month in the first year, what would that change?

**Tyler:** (laughs) That'd be a start, I guess. I'd be able to quit my job.

**Ștefan:** What do you do now?

> **[NODE: CS]**

**Tyler:** Sales rep. Inside sales, 50 hours, commission.

**Ștefan:** How do you feel about it?

**Tyler:** It's fine, but I'm not trying to be 40 and still on the phone. My boss is basically a clown.

**Ștefan:** What's the thing that makes you want out right now? Why now?

> **[NODE: EI]** The "why now" is where the insecurity lives.

**Tyler:** Honestly... I see my buddies doing better. One of them's got a nice place, a car. And I'm still renting with roommates. I'm just behind.

**Ștefan:** That's real. Comparison can sting. How long have you felt that?

**Tyler:** A year or so. It's been building.

**Ștefan:** How long are you hoping it takes to turn it around?

> **[NODE: TL]**

**Tyler:** Like a year. Six months if possible.

**Ștefan:** Have you traded before?

> **[NODE: PE]**

**Tyler:** Nah, I've watched all the big guys. I follow [INFLUENCER], [INFLUENCER]. I know how it works.

> **CURVEBALL: name-drop.** Don't comment on them.

**Ștefan:** Cool. You've seen a lot. What's kept you from starting for real?

> **[NODE: WGW]**

**Tyler:** I don't know where to start, honestly. Everyone's got a different system. And I don't have a big account.

**Ștefan:** So from what I understand, you're a sales rep working 50 hours, you want to get out and build something that gives you freedom and the lifestyle you've seen on others, you want about 10k a month in the first year to start, you've felt behind your friends, and what's in the way is you don't know where to start and who to trust, correct?

**Tyler:** Yeah, that's about right.

**Ștefan:** And are you committed to doing the work to get there?

**Tyler:** For sure.

> **Quick reality check.** "Doing the work" ≠ trading is easy.

**Ștefan:** One thing I want to be straight about, it's not a shortcut. It takes real time and practice. Are you good with that?

**Tyler:** Yeah, I know. I'll put the time in.

**Ștefan:** If there was a way to learn this properly with a mentor, would you be ready to invest in yourself?

**Tyler:** For sure.

**Ștefan:** It's a real mentorship, different programs, I wasn't trained on pricing. How much have you set aside?

**Tyler:** I could do about 2,500.

**Ștefan:** Great. I'll connect you with [CLOSER]. Sounds good?

**Tyler:** Let's do it.

**Ștefan:** Free tomorrow or Friday?

**Tyler:** Friday afternoon.

**Ștefan:** 3 or 4?

**Tyler:** 3.

**Ștefan:** Locked in Friday at 3. Best email?... Number?... Accept it now.

**Tyler:** Done.

**Ștefan:** If something comes up message me. I'll send a couple of videos, quiet place, computer.
