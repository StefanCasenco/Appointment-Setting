**Path:** INTENT → CS → G → TL → PE → WGW → GAP → URGENCY → FIN QUAL → PITCH → LOCK → SHOW UP
**Lead:** Ravi, 44, owns a small landscaping company with 6 employees. Seasonal income, wants another stream that isn't weather-dependent.
**Hidden truth:** Cash flow swings keep him up. He's also sceptical of anything that "feels like a pyramid".
**Curveballs:** he tries to run the call like a business meeting, and asks "what's the ROI?".
