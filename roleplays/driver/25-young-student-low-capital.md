# 25 · Young (18-22), Low Capital

**Path:** INTENT → CS → G → TL → PE → WGW → (early FIN QUAL) → honest exit
**Lead:** Jaden, 19, first-year college student. Works weekends at a gym, saves a little. Very motivated.
**Hidden truth:** He's smart and hungry, but doesn't have capital. He'd say yes to anything.
**Curveballs:** over-eagerness ("I'll do whatever it takes"), and a "my parents will pay" move.

> This one ends **without booking**, but with a path back in. The goal is to practise a kind, honest exit.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Jaden? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded our futures guide, right?

**Jaden:** Yes! That was me!

**Ștefan:** Cool, 10-15 minutes, to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. Sounds good?

**Jaden:** Yeah, absolutely.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Jaden:** I want to get into it early. I figure if I learn it now I'll be way ahead of everyone.

> **[NODE: INTENT → CS]**

**Ștefan:** I like that mindset. Are you studying right now?

**Jaden:** Yeah, first year. I also work part-time at a gym, about 15 hours.

**Ștefan:** How are you funding things day-to-day?

**Jaden:** Mostly the gym job and a bit from my parents. I'm trying to be independent.

> Early capital signal. Keep it light, but note it.

**Ștefan:** What do you want out of trading? What's the goal?

> **[NODE: G]**

**Jaden:** Make enough to not need a regular job. Like 3-5k a month.

**Ștefan:** When would you want that?

> **[NODE: TL]**

**Jaden:** By the time I graduate, three years.

**Ștefan:** Have you traded before?

> **[NODE: PE]**

**Jaden:** I practised on demo for about two months. I'm actually doing pretty well on demo.

**Ștefan:** That's great. What's the thing that's kept you from going live?

> **[NODE: WGW]**

**Jaden:** I only have about 300 saved. I don't want to risk it, and I don't know where to start properly.

**Ștefan:** So from what I understand, you're a first-year student working part-time, you want to learn early to be at 3-5k a month by graduation, you've done two months on demo, and what's stopped you is you've only got about 300 saved and don't know where to start, correct?

**Jaden:** Yeah, that's it.

> **Decision point.** Capital is far below the program. Don't pitch.

**Ștefan:** I appreciate you being straight. I'm going to be honest with you too, because I think it's the right thing to do. The mentorship we offer is a serious investment, and with where you are right now, I don't think it would be the right move.

**Jaden:** But I'll do whatever it takes. I'll work extra shifts.

> **CURVEBALL: over-eagerness.**

**Ștefan:** I believe you, and that drive will take you far. But I don't want you stretching to the point where you're stressed about money, because that's when people make bad trading decisions. A good plan is to keep building your savings and keep learning what you can for free.

**Jaden:** What if my parents help me pay?

> **CURVEBALL: parents.**

**Ștefan:** That's a conversation to have with them honestly, and I wouldn't ask them to cover something like this on a hunch. Let's put it this way: if you've got a solid plan, some savings, and you still want it, reach out in 6-12 months. Is that fair?

**Jaden:** ...Yeah. That's fair. I'd rather do it right.

**Ștefan:** That's the right attitude. In the meantime, keep reading, keep practising on demo, and focus on school. Take care, Jaden, I mean it.

**Jaden:** Thanks, Ștefan.
