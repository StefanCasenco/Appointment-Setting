# 18 · Family Provider

**Path:** INTENT → G → EI → CS → TL → PE → WGW → GAP → URGENCY → FIN QUAL → PITCH → LOCK → SHOW UP
**Lead:** Omar, 34, logistics coordinator. New baby (4 months), partner on parental leave, income dropped to one.
**Hidden truth:** He's terrified of failing as a provider. He doesn't want to miss the baby's early years because he's at work.
**Curveballs:** he gets emotional unexpectedly, and his baby cries mid-call.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Omar? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded our futures guide, right?

**Omar:** Yes, that was me.

**Ștefan:** Cool, 10-15 minutes, to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. Sounds good?

**Omar:** Sure, I've got a few minutes while she naps.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Omar:** We just had a baby. Money's tighter than it was and I need to find a way to earn more without giving up more time.

> **[NODE: INTENT → G]**

**Ștefan:** Congratulations, that's huge. How much more would you want to be making?

**Omar:** Maybe 2,000 a month on top of what I make. Honestly, it would take a lot of pressure off.

**Ștefan:** What would that let you do?

> **[NODE: EI]**

**Omar:** My wife could stay home a bit longer. I wouldn't be so stressed about daycare costs. I just want to be there for my daughter.

**Ștefan:** Why is that so important to you right now?

**Omar:** (pause) My dad worked all the time when I was a kid. I barely saw him. I don't want to be that dad.

> **CURVEBALL: he gets emotional.** Don't fill the silence, don't switch topics. One human beat.

**Ștefan:** That means a lot, man. That's a strong reason. How are you doing with that, balancing work and being around?

**Omar:** Tough. But it's what I've got.

**Ștefan:** What's your work like?

> **[NODE: CS]**

**Omar:** Logistics coordinator. 45 hours, some Saturdays. Pays okay, but we're stretching.

**Ștefan:** How long are you hoping to build this up?

> **[NODE: TL]**

**Omar:** A year, ideally. Before she goes back to work.

*(Baby cries in the background)*

> **CURVEBALL: interruption.**

**Omar:** Sorry, one sec... she's up.

**Ștefan:** No problem at all, take your time. Do you want me to call back in 10 minutes?

> Offer an easy out. Don't make him choose between you and his kid.

**Omar:** Yeah, can you? Maybe give me 15?

**Ștefan:** Of course. Talk soon.

*(15 minutes later)*

**Ștefan:** Hey Omar, it's Ștefan again. Good time?

**Omar:** Yeah, sorry about that. She's down now.

**Ștefan:** No worries at all. So we were talking about building this over the next year so your wife can stay home longer. Have you traded before?

> **[NODE: PE]**

**Omar:** No. I've read a lot, watched videos. I've been a bit afraid to start.

**Ștefan:** What's stopped you?

> **[NODE: WGW]**

**Omar:** Fear, honestly. I can't afford to lose money right now. And I don't have much time. I'd have to trade at odd hours.

**Ștefan:** So from what I understand, you've got a new baby, you want about 2,000 extra a month within a year so your wife can stay home and you can be present for your daughter, and what's stopped you is fear of losing money and not having a structured way to do it with limited time, correct?

**Omar:** Yes. That's it.

**Ștefan:** And are you ready to get started on this?

**Omar:** I think so. I want to do it right.

**Ștefan:** If there was a way to learn this in a structured way, with someone guiding you, would you be ready to invest in yourself?

**Omar:** Yes, but I'd need to talk to my wife.

**Ștefan:** Of course, that's smart. It's a real mentorship, different programs, I wasn't trained on pricing. How much have you got set aside?

**Omar:** About 3,000.

**Ștefan:** Okay. I'll connect you with [CLOSER], he'll go deeper, and if you want your wife on the call, that's fine too. Sounds good?

**Omar:** That'd be great.

**Ștefan:** Free tomorrow or Wednesday?

**Omar:** Wednesday evening, after the baby's down.

**Ștefan:** 8 or 9?

**Omar:** 8:30.

**Ștefan:** Locked in Wednesday at 8:30. Best email?... Number?... Accept it now.

**Omar:** Done.

**Ștefan:** If anything comes up, message me. I'll send a couple of videos, quiet place, computer.
