# 49 · Language Barrier

**Signal:** Can't follow the call or the coaching in the language it's delivered in.
**Lead:** Marco, 31, drives for a rideshare, limited English.
**Goal:** be patient, check understanding, and be clear about the program's language.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Marco? This is Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded the futures guide, is that right?

**Marco:** Yes, yes... the guide.

**Ștefan:** Great. I've got about 10 minutes, just to learn about you. Is that okay?

**Marco:** Okay.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading?

**Marco:** I want... money. More. For family.

> Slow down, short sentences, no idioms.

**Ștefan:** That's a good reason. How many hours do you work now?

**Marco:** Many. Sixty.

**Ștefan:** Okay. Have you tried trading before?

**Marco:** No. Only video. Is hard to understand, the English.

> **He told you the real barrier.**

**Ștefan:** I appreciate you telling me. Our calls and materials are in English. If your English is limited, it might be hard to follow the mentor. How comfortable are you with spoken English, from 1 to 10?

**Marco:** Five. Maybe four.

**Ștefan:** Thank you for being honest. For a program like this, where you need to follow a lot of detail, I'd be worried you won't get full value right now.

**Marco:** I understand.

**Ștefan:** What I'd suggest is keep learning English, especially trading vocabulary, and look for resources in your language. When you feel more comfortable, reach out.

**Marco:** Okay. Thank you.

**Ștefan:** Take care, Marco.

---

## Improv notes
- If your team offers materials or mentors in other languages, switch to that.
- Be patient, never condescending. Don't finish his sentences.
- If his English is fine and he just has an accent, don't disqualify. Check understanding with a recap question.
