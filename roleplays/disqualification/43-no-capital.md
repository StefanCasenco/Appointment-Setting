# 43 · No Capital

**Signal:** Nothing set aside, no income to spare, and no plan to get it.
**Lead:** Jess, 29, part-time barista. Genuinely motivated, genuinely broke.
**Goal of this call:** be kind, be honest, and don't send her to the closer for a conversation she can't act on.

---

**Ștefan:** Heeey Jess? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded the futures guide, right?

**Ștefan:** Cool, 10-15 minutes, to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. Sounds good?

**Jess:** Sure.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

> She told you the real obstacle. Don't pitch past it.

**Ștefan:** So from what I understand, you're a part-time barista, you want about 2k a month extra, you haven't started because you can't afford to risk money right now, correct?

**Ștefan:** If there was a way to learn, would you be ready to invest in yourself?

> **Decision point.**

**Ștefan:** I appreciate you telling me straight. The honest answer is that what we offer is a serious investment, and trading itself needs money you can afford to lose. With 150 dollars, I don't think the right move is for me to book you.

**Ștefan:** Here's what I'd suggest: focus on getting more hours and building a small cushion, even 500-1,000. Keep learning with free material. When you've got that, reach out, I'd be glad to help.

**Ștefan:** Take care, Jess.
