# 50 · No-Show / Ghost (Rebook Call)

**Signal:** Booked with the closer, didn't show, and is now answering again, or has gone silent.
**Lead:** Devon, 32, booked Thursday at 7, didn't show.
**Goal:** find out what happened without guilt-tripping, and either rebook with a stronger commitment or let go.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Hey Devon, Ștefan from [COMPANY]. We were meant to connect you with [CLOSER] on Thursday. Did something come up?

> No guilt trip. Open with curiosity.

**Devon:** Oh man, sorry. Work ran late, and I forgot to tell you.

**Ștefan:** No worries, it happens. Is this still something you want to pursue, or has your situation changed?

**Devon:** Still interested. I just lost track of the day.

**Ștefan:** Okay. Quick question so it doesn't happen again: what's the best time that's reliably free for you?

**Devon:** Weekends are better. Sunday afternoon.

**Ștefan:** Good. Before I rebook, can I ask what you were hoping to get out of the call, just so I'm sure it's still relevant?

> Re-confirm the reason. If it's gone vague, you'll hear it.

**Devon:** I want to get out of my job and trade. Same as before.

**Ștefan:** Okay. Let me be upfront, [CLOSER]'s slots are limited, and a no-show means someone else misses out. I'd like to lock Sunday at 2 if you're certain you can make it. Are you?

**Devon:** Yes. I'll be there.

**Ștefan:** If something does come up, will you message me before, even last minute?

**Devon:** Yeah, I will.

**Ștefan:** Great. Locked in Sunday at 2. Check your email and accept the invite now, while I'm on the line.

**Devon:** Done.

**Ștefan:** I'll text you Saturday evening and Sunday morning. And one more time, if you can't, message me and we'll move it. Deal?

**Devon:** Deal.

---

## If he'd ghosted instead

- Message once with a specific question: "Hey Devon, did something come up on Thursday? If you still want to talk, I can find a new time. If not, no worries."
- If no reply in 48 hours, send one final, short message, then close the lead.
- A second no-show is a no. Don't chase a third time.

## Improv notes
- If he'd said "I'm not sure I want to do this anymore", ask what changed. It might be doubt you can address, or a real no.
- Rebooks do best with a confirmation text the day before and the morning of.
- Track no-shows. If a lead repeats, be firm with the closer's time.
