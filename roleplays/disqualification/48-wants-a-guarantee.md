# 48 · Wants a Guarantee

**Signal:** Won't proceed without a promise of results or a money-back guarantee on profits.
**Lead:** Ken, 44, truck driver. Burned before, wants certainty.
**Goal:** be honest that no one can guarantee trading results, and see if he can live with that.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Ken? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded the futures guide, right?

**Ken:** Yep. First thing, is there a guarantee?

**Ștefan:** Good question, I'll answer it directly in a moment. Can I ask a couple things first so I know where you're coming from? 10-15 minutes.

**Ken:** Alright.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Ken:** I want a second income. 3k a month. I don't want to lose money.

**Ștefan:** What do you do now?   **Ken:** Trucker. 60 hours a week.

**Ștefan:** What's kept you from starting?   **Ken:** I don't want to risk it. Last time I tried, I lost 2k.

**Ștefan:** So from what I understand, you drive 60 hours, you want 3k a month, and you've been scared since losing 2k, correct?

**Ken:** Yes. So, will I make money or not?

> **CURVEBALL: the guarantee question, direct.**

**Ștefan:** I'll be honest with you, because you deserve a straight answer. Nobody can guarantee you'll make money trading, not us, not anyone. Anyone who does is lying to you. What a good mentor can do is teach you a process, hold you accountable, and help you manage risk. Can you live with that?

**Ken:** I was hoping for more than that.

**Ștefan:** I get it, you've been burned. If I told you what you wanted to hear, you'd have every reason to be angry later. What would it take for you to feel comfortable with the risk?

**Ken:** Honestly? A guarantee. Nothing else.

**Ștefan:** Then I think the honest answer is that this isn't going to be a fit right now. Trading always carries risk, and I don't want to pretend otherwise. If you reach a point where you're okay with that risk, you're welcome to reach out.

**Ken:** Fair enough. At least you were straight.

**Ștefan:** Take care, Ken.
