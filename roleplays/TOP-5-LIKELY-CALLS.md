# Top 5 Most Likely Trading Calls (short, casual version)

Five calls you're most likely to get on a trading offer, written the way the call should actually *sound*: short lines, casual, no form-reading. Same buckets as `../SETTING-SOP.md`, just spoken like a person.

## The call in your own words

This is the skeleton. Every call below is a variation of it.

**1. Open**
> "Hey [Name], this is Steven from [COMPANY], I think you booked in from the ad, is that right?"

**2. Frame (5 min, low pressure)**
> "Yeah brother, nothing too crazy, I've got like 5 min here. We've got a lot of students coming in, wanted to take a look at your application and see how we can support you, cool?"

**3. Why**
> "Ok, so tell me man, what got you looking into trading in the first place?"

**4. Context**
> "Give me some context on your life, like where you're at right now?"

**5. Goal**
> "Cool, so give me a quick idea of what you were hoping to achieve with trading. Where do you want to be in 6-12 months?"

**6. Then follow what they say.** Pick up the loaded bit and go one or two layers in, then:

- **Time:** "How long have you been looking into it?"
- **In the way:** "What's been the hold-up?"
- **Gap (their words):** "So you're [where they are], you want [where they want to be], and what's in the way is [X], right?"
- **Urgency:** "Is this something you want to sort now?"
- **Money, soft:** "Roughly, have you got something set aside you'd put towards this if it made sense?"
- **Book:** "Here's what I'd do, I'll get you on a call with [CLOSER], he'll look at where you're at and what makes sense. [Day] or [day]?"
- **Lock:** email, number, "accept the invite while I'm here", "if anything comes up, message me."

### Rules of the style

- **Short.** If a line is longer than two sentences, cut it.
- **React, then ask.** "Ah nice." "Gotcha." "Damn, okay." Then the question.
- **Only ask what comes from their last answer.**
- **Dig 2-3 layers, then stop.** "What's that look like?" "How so?" Echo their last words.
- **Never promise results.** No credit cards, borrowed or bill money.

`> PEEL` marks a dig, `> CURVEBALL` marks a surprise, `> [NODE]` marks the SOP bucket.

## 1 · Beginner who's sick of his job

**Lead:** Daniel, 27, warehouse nights, watches videos, no account. **Hidden truth:** barely sees his 3-year-old.

**Steven:** Hey Daniel, this is Steven from [COMPANY], I think you booked in from the ad, is that right?

**Daniel:** Yeah, that's me.

**Steven:** Nice. Nothing too crazy, I've got like 5 min here. We've got a lot of students coming in, wanted to take a look at your application and see how we can support you. Cool?

**Daniel:** Yeah, go on.

**Steven:** So what got you looking into trading in the first place?

**Daniel:** Just want to make a bit more, been watching videos for a while.

**Steven:** Gotcha. Give me some context on your life, where are you at right now?

**Daniel:** Warehouse, nights mostly.

> **PEEL 1**

**Steven:** Nights, how's that going?

**Daniel:** Tiring.

**Steven:** Tiring, what's that look like?

**Daniel:** Finish at six, sleep till two, my little lad's home from nursery, then I'm back at nine. I barely see him.

> **[NODE: EI]** He just gave you the real reason. Pause two seconds.

**Steven:** Damn. How's that sitting with you?

**Daniel:** Worst part. Feels like I'm missing it.

**Steven:** Yeah, I get that. So what were you hoping trading does for you? Where do you want to be in 6-12 months?

**Daniel:** Off nights. Maybe two and a half, three grand a month.

**Steven:** Cool. How long you been looking into it?

**Daniel:** Six months, just videos.

**Steven:** What's been the hold-up from going further?

**Daniel:** Don't know where to start, everyone says something different. And I can't afford to lose money.

> **CURVEBALL:** "Is this one of those get-rich things?"

**Steven:** Ha, fair. No. Nobody can promise results in trading and I'd stay away from anyone who does. What we do is give you a structure and someone to look at your work. Fair?

**Daniel:** Fair.

**Steven:** So you're on nights, barely seeing your lad, you want to be at about 3k a month so you can get off them, and what's in the way is not knowing where to start. Right?

**Daniel:** Yeah, that's it.

**Steven:** You want to sort this now?

**Daniel:** Yeah, I don't want to still be on nights in two years.

**Steven:** Roughly, you got something set aside you'd put towards it if it made sense? Not borrowed.

**Daniel:** Couple of grand maybe.

**Steven:** Good. Here's what I'd do, get you on a call with [CLOSER], he'll look at where you're at and what makes sense. Tomorrow or the day after?

**Daniel:** Day after.

**Steven:** Morning or afternoon? ... 3pm, locked. Best email? ... Number for a reminder? ... Accept the invite while I'm here. If anything comes up, message me.

**Handoff:** S0, Escape. Goal ~3k/mo to leave nights. Why: his son. Blocker: no starting point. Budget ~£2k, not borrowed. Booked day+2, 3pm.

## 2 · Paper trader stuck on "my strategy isn't ready"

**Lead:** Jordan, 23, bricklayer, paper trading 6 months. **Hidden truth:** scared to put real money in.

**Steven:** Hey Jordan, this is Steven from [COMPANY], I think you booked in from the ad, is that right?

**Jordan:** Yeah, just signed up.

**Steven:** Nice. I've got like 5 min here, nothing too crazy. We've got a lot of students coming in, wanted to look at your application and see how we can support you. Cool?

**Jordan:** Yeah, go for it.

**Steven:** So what got you into trading?

**Jordan:** Money, look after my family.

**Steven:** Gotcha. Give me some context on where you're at right now.

**Jordan:** Bricklaying, 50-60 hours with overtime. Paper trading for about six months.

**Steven:** Six months, nice, more than most. How's it going?

**Jordan:** Not great, I'm on and off. Don't have a strategy down.

> **PEEL 1**

**Steven:** Not having a strategy, what's that look like when you're sat there?

**Jordan:** I take trades that feel right, then second guess, or I watch a video and change everything.

**Steven:** Right. And how do you feel about the bricklaying?

**Jordan:** Don't enjoy it. It's trading my time for money.

**Steven:** Fair. So where do you want trading to take you in 6-12 months?

**Jordan:** Replace it eventually. Around 5k a month. I make about 3 now.

**Steven:** What's the hold-up from going paper to real?

**Jordan:** Just need to get my strategy down first.

> **CURVEBALL / PEEL 2:** test it gently, don't challenge.

**Steven:** Yeah, common one. If you had a strategy you trusted tomorrow, would you go live?

**Jordan:** ...Probably not straight away. I'd still be nervous about losing real money.

**Steven:** Ah, honest. So it's part strategy, part that first step of real money.

**Jordan:** Yeah, both.

**Steven:** So you're laying bricks 50-60 hours, six months in, want about 5k a month, and what's in the way is no solid strategy plus nerves about going live. Right?

**Jordan:** Yeah.

**Steven:** Want to get moving on it now?

**Jordan:** Yeah, I'm tired of waiting.

**Steven:** Roughly got something set aside you'd put towards it?

**Jordan:** Couple of hundred, a bit more if I had to.

> **Flag:** budget-sensitive. Don't quote a number. The closer decides what fits.

**Steven:** Thanks for being straight. I'll get you on a call with [CLOSER], he'll look at where you're at and what makes sense. Midweek or weekend?

**Jordan:** Weekend. Saturday, 11.

**Steven:** Locked. Best email? ... Number? ... Accept the invite while I'm here.

**Handoff:** S1/S2, Escape. Goal ~5k/mo to replace construction. Blocker: no strategy, nerves about live. Budget low (flag). Booked Sat 11.

## 3 · Prop-firm chaser who keeps failing challenges

**Lead:** Marcus, 29, IT support, 3 failed evals (~$450). **Hidden truth:** revenge trades, embarrassed.

**Steven:** Hey Marcus, this is Steven from [COMPANY], I think you booked in from the ad, is that right?

**Marcus:** Yeah.

**Steven:** Nice. I've got like 5 min here, nothing too crazy. We've got a lot of students coming in, wanted to take a look at your application and see how we can support you. Cool?

**Marcus:** Sure.

**Steven:** So what got you into trading?

**Marcus:** Trying to get funded. Tried a few prop challenges, not through yet.

**Steven:** Gotcha. How many?

**Marcus:** Three.

> **PEEL 1**

**Steven:** What happened on those?

**Marcus:** Got close to the target, started pushing, took extra trades. Hit the daily loss.

**Steven:** And then?

**Marcus:** Broke my rules. Revenge traded.

> **[NODE: EI]** He said the real problem. Don't pounce.

**Steven:** Yeah, super common. How'd that feel?

**Marcus:** Embarrassing. About $450 gone and I knew what to do.

**Steven:** *(pause)* Knowing it and doing it under pressure aren't the same.

> **CURVEBALL:** "One of them was the platform glitching."

**Steven:** Ha, annoying. Did that one change the result, or would it have happened anyway?

**Marcus:** ...Probably happened anyway. Bad week already.

**Steven:** Give me some context, where are you at outside of trading?

**Marcus:** IT support. It's fine, not going anywhere.

**Steven:** Cool. Where do you want to be in 6-12 months?

**Marcus:** Funded, trading for a living. Four, five k a month to start.

**Steven:** How long you been at it?

**Marcus:** Year and a half, on and off.

**Steven:** So eighteen months, three challenges, and the thing that keeps tripping you is sticking to the rules under pressure. Right?

**Marcus:** Yeah.

**Steven:** Want to sort that now, or keep running challenges?

**Marcus:** I don't want to keep burning money.

**Steven:** Roughly got savings you'd put towards coaching, separate from challenge fees?

**Marcus:** Some. Depends what it is.

**Steven:** Fair, that's what the next call's for. I'll get you on with [CLOSER], he'll look at your last few challenges and what makes sense. Tomorrow or Thursday?

**Marcus:** Thursday evening.

**Steven:** 6 or 7? ... 7, locked. Best email? ... Number? ... Accept the invite. Have your journal to hand if you've got one.

**Handoff:** S4/S7. 3 failed evals. Blocker: breaks rules near target and when down. Goal 4-5k/mo. Has savings, amount unknown. Booked Thu 7pm.

## 4 · Price-first skeptic

**Lead:** Priya, 34, marketing manager, burned by a past course. **Hidden truth:** not against paying, scared of being scammed again.

**Steven:** Hey Priya, this is Steven from [COMPANY], I think you booked in from the ad, is that right?

**Priya:** Yeah. Before we start, how much is this going to cost?

> **CURVEBALL:** price up front. Park it with a reason.

**Steven:** Ha, straight to it. I'm the first conversation, I wasn't trained on pricing and there are different programs depending on where people are at, so I'd probably give you the wrong number. Mind if I ask a couple things first?

**Priya:** Okay. But I don't want to be sold to.

**Steven:** Good, I'm not here to. I've got like 5 min, we've got a lot of students coming in, wanted to take a look at your application and see how we can support you. Cool?

**Priya:** Cool.

**Steven:** So what got you looking into trading?

**Priya:** A friend does it and seems to do well. But I'm wary, I did a course years ago and wasted money.

> **PEEL 1**

**Steven:** Ugh, what happened?

**Priya:** All hype, no real help, and when I asked questions I got a sales page.

**Steven:** Yeah, I'd be wary too. What would you need to see to feel okay about this?

> **PEEL 2:** her worry becomes her criteria.

**Priya:** A real person. Honest about risk. No hype.

> **CURVEBALL:** "Is this a scam?"

**Steven:** Fair. Trading can lose you money, that's the risk, full stop. And you'll be talking to a person. Does that help?

**Priya:** It does, actually.

**Steven:** Give me some context, where are you at right now?

**Priya:** Marketing manager, decent salary. Want a second income to speed up saving for a house.

**Steven:** Cool. Where do you want to be in 6-12 months?

**Priya:** A few hundred extra a week would be great.

**Steven:** How long you been thinking about it?

**Priya:** About a year.

**Steven:** What's been the hold-up?

**Priya:** Fear of getting it wrong again. And not knowing who to trust.

**Steven:** So you want a second income to speed up the house, a few hundred a week, and what's stopped you is not knowing who to trust after the last course. Right?

**Priya:** Yeah.

**Steven:** Want to get moving on it?

**Priya:** If it's the right thing, yes.

**Steven:** Roughly, got something set aside? I'm asking so we don't waste your time, not to price you.

**Priya:** Yes. I'd rather not say a number yet.

> Respect it. Note: "has budget, guarded".

**Steven:** No problem. I'll get you on a call with [CLOSER], he'll answer the pricing and "what's the catch" questions properly. If it's not for you you've lost nothing. Day after tomorrow, evening?

**Priya:** Yes. I'll bring questions.

**Steven:** Perfect. 6 or 7? ... 6, locked. Best email? ... Number? ... Accept the invite.

**Handoff:** S0/S1, Skeptic/Price-first, Supplement. Goal: few hundred a week for house savings. Burned by past course. Criteria: real person, honest about risk. Budget exists, number not given. Booked day+2, 6pm.

## 5 · The one under money pressure

**Lead:** Chloe, 26, part-time retail, behind on rent. **Hidden truth:** nothing to risk, hoping trading is a quick fix.

**Steven:** Hey Chloe, this is Steven from [COMPANY], I think you booked in from the ad, is that right?

**Chloe:** Yeah. I really need this to work.

**Steven:** Nice. I've got like 5 min here, wanted to take a look at your application and see how we can support you. Cool?

**Chloe:** Please.

**Steven:** So what got you looking into trading?

**Chloe:** I'm behind on rent, in my overdraft. I thought if I could trade properly I could get on top of it fast.

> **[NODE: EI]** Loaded. Go human first.

**Steven:** Damn, that sounds stressful. How long's it been like this?

**Chloe:** Six months. Keeps getting worse.

**Steven:** Sorry to hear that. Give me some context, what are you doing for work?

**Chloe:** Retail, part-time. Barely covers it.

**Steven:** And what were you hoping to achieve with trading?

**Chloe:** A grand or two a month, as fast as possible.

**Steven:** Have you traded before?

**Chloe:** Never. Watched a few videos.

> **[NODE: QUAL, honest]** Pressure plus no capital is a hard stop.

**Steven:** I want to be upfront, I'd rather be honest than just book you in. Is there any money set aside you could risk without touching rent or bills?

**Chloe:** Not really. I was thinking of putting it on my credit card.

> **CURVEBALL:** credit card. HARD STOP. Clear, kind, final.

**Steven:** I'm going to be straight with you because I care how this goes. I wouldn't use a credit card for this, or money you need for rent. Trading can lose money, and it's harder when you *have* to make it back. I don't think booking you in is the right move right now.

**Chloe:** *(quiet)* Okay.

**Steven:** Not never. If you can build a small cushion that isn't rent money and things ease off, come back and I'd happily talk then. In the meantime, a free debt advice service could help you more, and with less risk. Want me to point you to one?

**Chloe:** Yeah. I didn't think of that.

**Steven:** I really hope it turns around. Message me anytime.

**Handoff:** Not booked. S0, Pressure. Behind on rent, in overdraft, no savings, suggested credit card (declined). Pointed to free debt advice. Can return if circumstances change.

## Practise

1. Read one, then cover the lead's lines and answer as yourself.
2. Run it with a partner using only the lead line and the hidden truth.
3. Cut every line down to its shortest version.
4. Count your questions. Every one should come from the last answer.
5. Change the curveball and see if you still land it in 5-8 minutes.
