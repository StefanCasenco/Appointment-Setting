# 05 · Inconsistent

**Path:** INTENT → PE → EI → G → CS → TL → WGW → GAP → URGENCY → FIN QUAL → PITCH → LOCK → SHOW UP
**Lead:** Jordan, 38, IT support. 3 years trading. Profitable some months, blows it back others.
**Hidden truth:** One bad month last quarter erased four good ones. Stress shows up at home.
**Curveballs:** "I don't need a mentor, I just need discipline", and a partner-approval stall at the end.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Jordan? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded our futures guide, right?

**Jordan:** That's me.

**Ștefan:** Cool, I've got 10-15 minutes, to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. Cool?

**Jordan:** Fine.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Jordan:** I want to be consistent. I'm trading already, but one week I'm up, next week I'm down.

> **[NODE: INTENT → PE]** "Inconsistent" = script says PAST EXPERIENCE right away.

**Ștefan:** When you say inconsistent, what do you mean exactly? How long have you been trading?

**Jordan:** Three years. I trade ES. Last year I made about 9k, but I gave back nearly 6 of it over two months.

**Ștefan:** So you can get green, but the drawdowns eat it?

**Jordan:** That's it.

> **[NODE: PE]** Real numbers = easy. He's specific, don't over-dig.

**Ștefan:** What does that do to you when it happens? The give-back months.

> **[NODE: EI]** Per his order, impact comes right after PE.

**Jordan:** It wrecks me. I get irritable. I stop sleeping. My wife says I'm a different person during those weeks.

**Ștefan:** That's a heavy price to pay. Is that part of what you're trying to solve?

**Jordan:** Yeah. It's not just the money, it's what it does to the house.

**Ștefan:** I hear you. What would "consistent" look like to you? What do you want to be making?

> **[NODE: G]**

**Jordan:** Let's say 5k a month, but steady. No big drops.

**Ștefan:** And what's the plan with 5k? Quit the job, keep both?

**Jordan:** I'd keep the job for a while but it'd give me options. Eventually get out.

**Ștefan:** What do you do at the job?

> **[NODE: CS]**

**Jordan:** IT support, 40 hours. It's comfortable. Pays about 70k.

**Ștefan:** How do you feel about it?

**Jordan:** It's fine. But it's a ceiling. I don't want to be there at 50.

**Ștefan:** How long have you been trying to fix the inconsistency?

> **[NODE: TL → WGW]**

**Jordan:** Over a year, I'd say. Since the first big drawdown.

**Ștefan:** And what do you feel has gotten in the way of fixing it?

**Jordan:** Honestly? Discipline. I know what to do. I just don't do it when I'm in the trade.

> **CURVEBALL: "I don't need a mentor, I need discipline."**

**Jordan:** I don't know if a mentor would help. It's psychology. I don't need a strategy.

**Ștefan:** Fair, and I'm not here to tell you that you do. Quick question: when you break your rules, is it in the same situations, like after a loss, or when you feel you need to make it back?

**Jordan:** ...After a loss, yeah. Revenge trading.

**Ștefan:** So there's a pattern. Have you had someone looking at that pattern with you, or is it just you with your journal?

**Jordan:** Just me.

> Don't argue "you need a mentor". Let him realise "just me" is the gap.

**Ștefan:** So from what I understand, you're in a steady job, trading for three years, you can get green but a couple bad months erase it, it's wrecking your sleep and your home, you want a steady 5k a month with fewer drops, and the thing in the way is the pattern after losses that you've been trying to fix alone, right?

**Jordan:** Yeah. That's the picture.

**Ștefan:** And are you actually committed to fixing it now, or is it something you'll keep chipping at?

**Jordan:** I'm done chipping at it. I'd rather get it fixed.

**Ștefan:** Good. If there was a way to get that fixed with someone watching your trades, would you be ready to invest in yourself?

**Jordan:** I'd consider it. I need to talk to my wife first though.

> **CURVEBALL: spouse.** Don't push past it. Make her part of the plan.

**Ștefan:** Of course. Do you think she'd be supportive, given how much this has been weighing on you both?

**Jordan:** Probably. She wants me back to normal.

**Ștefan:** Then I wouldn't try to guess. It's not a $50 course, it's a real mentorship, different programs, and I wasn't trained on pricing. How much have you set aside to put toward getting this right?

**Jordan:** I could put 5-6k toward it if it made sense.

**Ștefan:** Great. I'll connect you with [CLOSER]. He'll go deeper into you, and if it's a fit, he'll walk you through it. If your wife wants to join the call, that's welcome too. Sounds good?

**Jordan:** I'll ask her.

**Ștefan:** Are you free tomorrow or Thursday?

**Jordan:** Thursday evening.

**Ștefan:** 7 or 8?

**Jordan:** 8.

**Ștefan:** Locked in Thursday at 8. Best email?... Number for a reminder?... Accept the invite for me.

**Jordan:** Done.

**Ștefan:** If anything comes up, message me and we'll move it. I'll send some videos, watch them, quiet place, computer.
