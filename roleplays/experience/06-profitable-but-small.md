# 06 · Profitable but Small

**Path:** INTENT → PE → G → CS → TL → WGW → GAP → URGENCY → FIN QUAL → PITCH → LOCK → SHOW UP
**Lead:** Liam, 27, junior accountant. Makes about $500/month trading a $5k account. Wants to quit his job at $7k/month.
**Hidden truth:** He's scared to size up because of one big loss early on. Money isn't the barrier, fear is.
**Curveballs:** "everyone wants more money" riff, then a mid-call deflection ("I'm busy, can we do this later?").

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Liam? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded our guide about trading futures, that right?

**Liam:** Yeah, that's me.

**Ștefan:** Cool, 10-15 minutes, just to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. Cool?

**Liam:** Sure.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Liam:** I'm already trading, I'm profitable, just not making as much as I want.

> **[NODE: INTENT → PE]** Profitable = take it seriously, ask for numbers.

**Ștefan:** Nice, that's more than most. How long have you traded, and when you say profitable, what does that look like?

**Liam:** About two years. Account's 5k, I make around 500 a month, sometimes 600.

**Ștefan:** Okay, so about a 10% month on a small account, that's not nothing.

**Liam:** Right? But it doesn't pay my bills.

> Honest recognition. Don't flatter, just show you hear the number.

**Ștefan:** What would you want to be making, to feel like it's worth it?

> **[NODE: G]**

**Liam:** 7,000 a month. That's what I'd need to quit.

**Ștefan:** That's a big jump. How far do you feel you are from that?

**Liam:** Far. Like very far.

**Ștefan:** Okay. When you say very far, is that a skills thing, a capital thing, a time thing?

**Liam:** A bit of everything. I mostly don't have a bigger account.

> Don't fall for "capital". Keep asking.

**Ștefan:** What do you do for work?

> **[NODE: CS]**

**Liam:** Junior accountant. Busy season is killing me.

**Ștefan:** How many hours?

**Liam:** 55 in busy season. Normal 45.

**Ștefan:** And you want out of that.

**Liam:** Yeah, I'm not an accountant at heart. My parents pushed me.

> A side-motivation appeared. Note it, don't chase it unless it connects.

**Ștefan:** How long are you hoping it takes to get from 500 to 7,000?

> **[NODE: TL]**

**Liam:** Ideally 12 months. Realistically? I don't know.

**Ștefan:** What do you think has kept you at 500 for two years?

> **[NODE: WGW]**

**Liam:** I don't size up. I know I should, but I get nervous. Early on I lost 1,500 in a day on a bad trade and I've been scared since.

**Ștefan:** That's honest. So it's not that you don't know how, it's the confidence to scale it?

**Liam:** Yeah. And I guess I don't have anyone to tell me it's okay.

> **CURVEBALL: deflection.**

**Liam:** Look, I'm kind of in the middle of something, can we do this later?

**Ștefan:** Totally, I can do two minutes now and then see if we need to book something. Is that okay? I just want to make sure it makes sense for you.

> Don't beg. Offer a shorter path.

**Liam:** Yeah, two minutes.

**Ștefan:** Cool. So from what I understand, you're a junior accountant, 45-55 hours a week, trading two years at about 500 a month, you want 7,000 so you can quit, and what's in the way is you don't size up because of that early loss, you don't have someone to guide you through scaling, correct?

**Liam:** Yeah, that's it.

**Ștefan:** And do you actually want to fix that now?

**Liam:** Yes. I'm tired of being stuck at 500.

**Ștefan:** If there was a way to get you scaling safely with a mentor, would you be ready to invest in yourself, if that's what it took?

**Liam:** Depends. What's the price?

**Ștefan:** I wasn't trained on pricing, there are different programs depending on where people are, and I'd rather not quote the wrong one. How much have you set aside to invest in this?

**Liam:** I could do about 4,000.

**Ștefan:** Okay. I'll connect you with [CLOSER], he'll go deeper, and if it's a fit he'll walk you through it. Sounds good?

**Liam:** Sure.

**Ștefan:** Are you free tomorrow or Friday?

**Liam:** Friday after 6.

**Ștefan:** 6:30 or 7:30?

**Liam:** 7:30.

**Ștefan:** Locked in Friday 7:30. Best email?... Number?... Accept it now for me.

**Liam:** Got it.

**Ștefan:** If something comes up, message me. I'll send you a couple of videos, watch them, quiet place, computer.
