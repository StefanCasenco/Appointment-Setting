# 10 · Blown-Account Survivor

**Path:** INTENT → PE → EI → CS → G → TL → WGW → GAP → URGENCY → FIN QUAL → PITCH → LOCK → SHOW UP
**Lead:** Sam, 33, bartender. Blew a $9k account in six weeks last spring. Hasn't traded since.
**Hidden truth:** The loss was most of his savings. He's ashamed, but he also feels he can't walk away without proving himself.
**Curveballs:** emotional dump, then an offhand "should I even be doing this?" that tempts you to push.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Sam? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded the futures guide, that right?

**Sam:** Yeah. Honestly I don't know why I did.

**Ștefan:** That's fine. 10-15 minutes, to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. Alright?

**Sam:** Sure.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Sam:** I traded last year and blew my account. I wanted to try again, I guess.

> **[NODE: INTENT → PE]**

**Ștefan:** That's tough. What happened?

**Sam:** I had about 9k. Over six weeks it went to nothing. I was overtrading, doubling up. It was bad.

**Ștefan:** Wow. I'm sorry man. How have you been since then?

> **[NODE: EI]** Go straight in, it's the real topic.

**Sam:** Not great. That was most of my savings. I barely told anybody. My brother knows, that's it.

**Ștefan:** Yeah, that's a lot to carry on your own. How are you feeling about trading now? Do you still want it?

**Sam:** Yeah. Part of me wants to prove it wasn't a waste. Part of me thinks I'm an idiot for even looking again.

> **CURVEBALL: "should I even be doing this?"** Don't push or reassure with hype. Be honest and curious.

**Sam:** Honestly... should I even be doing this?

**Ștefan:** That's a fair question, and I can't answer it for you yet. Let me understand where you're at and what you actually want, then we'll see if it makes sense. Is that okay?

**Sam:** Yeah. Okay.

**Ștefan:** What do you do for work these days?

> **[NODE: CS]**

**Sam:** Bartender. Nights. About 40 hours.

**Ștefan:** How do you feel about that?

**Sam:** It pays the bills. I'm worn out though. I'm 33, I don't want to be doing this at 40.

**Ștefan:** What's your financial situation like right now? Rebuilding savings?

**Sam:** Slowly. I've got about 1,500 saved again.

> Important flag: capital is low. Note it.

**Ștefan:** What would you want out of trading this time?

> **[NODE: G]**

**Sam:** Just steady money, 2,000 a month. Not to get rich. I'm done with the dreams of getting rich.

**Ștefan:** I like that, that's a realistic number. When would you want to be at 2,000?

> **[NODE: TL]**

**Sam:** A year. Maybe two.

**Ștefan:** What do you feel got in the way last time? Being honest.

> **[NODE: WGW]**

**Sam:** No rules. I was winging it. I'd lose, then try to get it back. I didn't have a plan or someone to say stop.

**Ștefan:** So no risk rules and nobody holding you accountable. So from what I understand, you're a bartender working nights, you lost most of your savings last year, you've got about 1,500 saved back up, you want about 2,000 a month in steady income within a year or two, and what got in the way was having no rules and no one holding you to them, correct?

**Sam:** Yeah. That's the whole story.

**Ștefan:** And are you really ready to try this again, properly this time?

**Sam:** I think so. I'm scared, but yes.

> Honest. Now FIN QUAL, with care.

**Ștefan:** If there was a way to learn how to do this properly with some guidance, would you be ready to invest in yourself, if that's what it took?

**Sam:** I'd like to. But I don't have much right now.

**Ștefan:** I appreciate you being straight. It's a real mentorship, different programs, and I wasn't trained on pricing. Just so I can be honest about whether it's a fit, how much do you have set aside that's *not* needed for rent and bills?

**Sam:** Realistically? Maybe 500-700 right now. The rest is my emergency fund.

> **Decision point.** Low capital + recent big loss. Don't push him past what he can afford.

**Ștefan:** I really appreciate that, and I won't tell you to touch your emergency fund. That would be the wrong move. Here's what I can do. I'd rather be honest, with that budget [CLOSER] may not have a program that fits right now, but he can look at your situation and tell you if there's an option, or what a realistic path looks like. No pressure. Would that be okay?

**Sam:** Yeah. That's fair.

**Ștefan:** Are you free tomorrow or Thursday?

**Sam:** Thursday, before my shift.

**Ștefan:** 3 or 4?

**Sam:** 3.

**Ștefan:** Locked in Thursday at 3. Best email?... Number?... Accept it for me.

**Sam:** Done.

**Ștefan:** If anything comes up, message me. I'll send a couple of videos, quiet place, computer. And Sam, thanks for being honest today.
