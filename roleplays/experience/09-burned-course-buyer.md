# 09 · Burned Course Buyer

**Path:** INTENT → PE → EI → WGW → G → TL → CS → GAP → URGENCY → FIN QUAL → PITCH → LOCK → SHOW UP
**Lead:** Rob, 42, HVAC technician. Spent $2,400 on a "mentorship" that turned out to be a Discord full of signals and no support.
**Hidden truth:** He's angry mostly at himself for falling for it. He wants trading to work but trusts nobody.
**Curveballs:** "How do I know you're not the same?", and "send me proof".

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Rob? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded our futures guide, right?

**Rob:** Yeah. I'm skeptical of all this stuff, to be honest.

**Ștefan:** I'd be too. 10-15 minutes, to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. If it doesn't make sense, I'll tell you. Fair?

**Rob:** Okay.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Rob:** I was in a program before. Paid for it. It was garbage.

> **[NODE: INTENT → PE]** The pain is the intent. Go directly into what happened.

**Ștefan:** I'm sorry about that, man. What happened?

**Rob:** Paid 2,400. Got access to a Discord with signals. Nobody answered questions. A few calls that were just them selling me the next program.

**Ștefan:** That's frustrating. Are you still trading today?

**Rob:** On and off. Small account.

**Ștefan:** What's that been like since?

> **[NODE: EI]**

**Rob:** Lost confidence. Honestly feel stupid. My wife says I wasted money, she's not wrong.

**Ștefan:** I don't think you were stupid, I think you trusted the wrong people. A lot of people have been there. What do you think was missing in that program?

> **[NODE: WGW]**

**Rob:** Someone who actually looked at my trades. Real feedback. A plan, not "buy when I say buy".

**Ștefan:** That makes sense. If you had that, what would you want out of trading?

> **[NODE: G]**

**Rob:** Honestly, a second income. 2-3k a month. I do HVAC, it's wearing my body down. I don't want to be on a roof when I'm 55.

**Ștefan:** Totally. Is that the plan, supplement, or replace?

**Rob:** Eventually replace. But supplement first.

> **CURVEBALL: "How do I know you're not the same?"**

**Rob:** And how do I know you guys aren't just another one of those? I'm not paying for another thing that disappears.

**Ștefan:** That's the right question. You shouldn't take my word for it. That's why the next step isn't about paying anyone, it's a conversation with [CLOSER], and part of that is you asking him exactly what you were missing last time. What would you need to see to feel okay about something like this?

> Answer with a question. Let him define his own trust criteria.

**Rob:** Real support. People I can ask. And no "pay more for the real stuff".

**Ștefan:** That's fair. I'll make sure that's what we discuss. How long have you been looking at trading in total?

> **[NODE: TL]**

**Rob:** About two years. The program was last year.

**Ștefan:** And what's your day look like now? Work-wise?

> **[NODE: CS]**

**Rob:** HVAC, 50 hours. Physical. Honestly I'd love to do less.

**Ștefan:** So from what I understand, you're an HVAC tech working 50 physical hours, you've been in trading for two years, you got burned by a program that gave you signals and no support, you want 2-3k a month on the side and eventually to replace the job, and what's missing is real feedback from someone who'll actually look at your trading, correct?

**Rob:** Yeah. That's it.

**Ștefan:** And are you actually ready to take another shot at this, or is it too soon after the last one?

**Rob:** If it's real, I'm ready. If it's another scam, I'm not.

**Ștefan:** That's fair. If there was a way to get that support, would you be ready to invest in yourself again, if it was the right fit?

**Rob:** If it's the right fit. I'm not rich.

**Ștefan:** I get it. It's a real mentorship, different programs, and I wasn't trained on pricing because it depends on where you are. How much could you comfortably put toward it without hurting your family?

**Rob:** Maybe 2,000. That's the limit.

> Honest, bounded. Pass that to the closer ("budget limit, trust issue").

**Ștefan:** Okay, I appreciate your honesty. I'll connect you with [CLOSER]. Ask him anything, I mean anything. Sound good?

**Rob:** Okay.

**Ștefan:** Are you free tomorrow or Wednesday?

**Rob:** Wednesday evening.

**Ștefan:** 6 or 7?

**Rob:** 7.

**Ștefan:** Locked in Wednesday at 7. Best email?... Number?... Accept it for me now.

**Rob:** Done.

**Ștefan:** I'll send a couple of videos, watch them, quiet place, computer. If something comes up message me.
