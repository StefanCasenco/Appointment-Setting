# 02 · Watcher / Content Learner

**Path:** INTENT → PE → G → CS → TL → WGW → GAP → URGENCY → FIN QUAL → PITCH → LOCK → SHOW UP
**Lead:** Priya, 24, junior marketing exec. Has watched "hundreds of hours" of YouTube, follows five trading accounts, never put a real dollar in.
**Hidden truth:** She's drowning in conflicting strategies and uses "more learning" to avoid starting.
**Curveballs:** she over-explains (info dump) and tries to end the call early with "just send me the info".

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Priya? It's Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded our futures guide, is that right?

**Priya:** Oh yeah! I did, a few days ago.

**Ștefan:** Cool, I've got 10-15 minutes, reason for the call is to understand where you're at, where you wanna be and, if I can help, point you in the right direction. Cool?

**Priya:** Sure.

**Ștefan:** So tell me, what had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Priya:** So I've been learning about it for like a year. I watch ICT, I watch a few other guys, I've gone through supply and demand, order blocks, liquidity, I started on this other channel that says only use price action but then another says indicators... I just find it fascinating, honestly. And I want to be good at it.

> **[NODE: INTENT → PE]** Huge info dump. Don't try to process it all. Pull ONE thread: "a year" + "haven't started"?

**Ștefan:** Wow, a year, that's serious. Out of all of that, have you actually traded anything live, or is it mostly learning so far?

**Priya:** Demo only. I don't want to start until I know what I'm doing.

**Ștefan:** Makes sense. What's the end goal though? When you say "be good at it", what does good look like for you?

**Priya:** Like, consistently profitable. I'd love to do it on the side and eventually go full-time.

> **[NODE: G, partial]** "Eventually go full-time", a real goal is hiding there. Park it, ask later.

**Ștefan:** Okay, full-time as in leave your job?

**Priya:** Yeah. Marketing is okay but I'm not in love with it.

**Ștefan:** What do you do there, hours-wise?

**Priya:** Normal 9 to 6, sometimes more. Not terrible.

**Ștefan:** How much would you need to make trading for leaving to be worth it?

**Priya:** Honestly if I could replace my salary, so about 3,500 a month.

> **CURVEBALL (early exit): "Could you just send me the info?"** She senses a sales call.

**Priya:** Actually, can you just email me whatever you've got? I'm a bit busy and I learn better reading.

**Ștefan:** Totally, I'll send you stuff. Can I ask one thing first so I send you the right thing, not random stuff? You've been learning for a year and you're still on demo, what's your honest take on what's kept you from going live?

> Reframe: you aren't blocking her, you're making the email useful. Gets WGW without a pitch.

**Priya:** (pause) I think... I never feel ready. Every time I find a strategy I like, another video says it's wrong. So I go learn something else.

**Ștefan:** That cycle's super common. So you know a lot, but nothing's ever locked into one system you trust?

**Priya:** Yes. Exactly. I have like 40 notes files and no plan.

> **[NODE: WGW]** Obstacle = no single system + nobody to say "this one". Perfect.

**Ștefan:** How long has that been going on?

**Priya:** About a year, like I said.

**Ștefan:** A year of watching and not starting. How does that feel?

> **[NODE: EI, late]** Pain surfaced here, take it when it comes.

**Priya:** Frustrating, honestly. I feel like I'm wasting time. My friend started last year and he's already funded.

**Ștefan:** So from what I understand, you're in a good job, learning for a year but still on demo, you want to replace your salary, about 3,500 a month, and the thing holding you back is you've got information everywhere but no single system or guidance that tells you "do this", correct?

**Priya:** Yeah that's exactly it.

**Ștefan:** And you told me you want to go full-time eventually, are you actually committed to making that happen now, or is it more of a hobby for now?

**Priya:** No, I want to make it real. I don't want another year of watching.

**Ștefan:** Love it. If there was a way to actually get you to a point where you trade one system with someone checking your work, would you be ready to invest in yourself if that's what it took?

**Priya:** Probably, what are we talking about?

**Ștefan:** It's not a $50 course, it's a real mentorship, there are different programs depending on where you start and how much support you want. I wasn't trained on pricing, mostly because it'd be unfair to quote you the wrong one. How much would you say you've got set aside to invest in getting this right?

**Priya:** I've got savings, I could put a few thousand towards it.

**Ștefan:** Great. Here's what I can do, I'll connect you with [CLOSER], senior mentor, he'll go deeper into your situation, and if it's a fit he'll walk you through it. Sounds good?

**Priya:** Okay, yeah.

**Ștefan:** Are you free tomorrow or Friday?

**Priya:** Friday after work.

**Ștefan:** 6:30 or 7:30?

**Priya:** 7.

**Ștefan:** Locked in, Friday at 7. Best email?... Number good for a reminder text?... Check your inbox for me and accept it.

**Priya:** Done.

**Ștefan:** If something comes up message me and we'll move it, yeah? I'll send you a couple of videos, watch those, then find a quiet spot on a computer.

**Priya:** Will do.

**Ștefan:** Perfect, take care.
