# 01 · Total Beginner

**Path:** INTENT → G → CS → EI → TL → WGW → GAP → URGENCY → FIN QUAL → PITCH → LOCK → SHOW UP
**Lead:** Daniel, 29, retail shift supervisor. Never placed a live trade. Watched a few videos, downloaded the futures guide on a Sunday night.
**Hidden truth:** He's been "looking into it" for 7 months and scared of losing money he can't replace. Real driver is wanting to stop being the guy who's always short at month end.
**Curveballs in this call:** a quick "is this legit?" and a price question at the financial step.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Daniel? This is Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded our guide about possibly starting to trade futures, is that right?

**Daniel:** Uh yeah, yeah that was me.

**Ștefan:** Cool, listen I've got 10-15 minutes here, reason for the call is just to get an understanding of where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I think I can help I can point you in the right direction. Sounds good?

**Daniel:** Yeah sure.

**Ștefan:** Okay, so tell me man, what had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Daniel:** Honestly just to make some extra money. I've seen a lot of people do it.

> **[NODE: INTENT]** Vague ("extra money"). Dig, don't accept it. Also: "I've seen people do it" = beginner, skip PAST EXP except one quick confirm later.

**Ștefan:** Sure, everyone wants extra money, I've never met anyone who wants less, right? Why do you want it right now though?

**Daniel:** (laughs) Fair. I guess... I'm always cutting it close by the end of the month. Like I work, I'm not broke, but there's nothing left.

> **[NODE: G, partial]** Pain showed up already. Stay with it, don't jump to the next question.

**Ștefan:** Yeah that's a stressful way to live, checking the balance before you buy groceries. So if trading worked out, how much would you need to be making for it to actually be worth your time?

**Daniel:** Maybe like 1,500 a month on top? That'd change everything.

**Ștefan:** That would. What would that let you do that you can't do right now?

**Daniel:** Not stress, mostly. And maybe finally move out of my mum's spare room, I'm 29, it's embarrassing.

> **[NODE: EI]** Came out on its own. Real why = independence/dignity. Note it, you'll reuse "not stressing at month end" in the gap.

**Ștefan:** No, man, a lot of people are in that spot, it's not embarrassing, it's a move you're trying to make. So what do you do for a living right now?

**Daniel:** Shift supervisor at a supermarket.

**Ștefan:** Okay. How do you feel about that job?

**Daniel:** It's fine. Stable. But it's the ceiling, you know? Nowhere to go from there.

**Ștefan:** How many hours a week?

**Daniel:** Like 45, nights and weekends mixed.

**Ștefan:** Okay so basically your main goal is to build a second income, around 1,500 a month, so you can move out and stop watching the balance every month, is that right?

**Daniel:** Yeah, that's it exactly.

> **[NODE: CS → G confirmed]** Did the confirm-back out loud. Notice you never asked "how much do you earn", it can wait or come at FIN QUAL.

**Ștefan:** How long have you been looking into trading?

**Daniel:** About seven months, on and off.

**Ștefan:** Seven months, man. Have you actually placed any trades?

**Daniel:** No, just demo. I watch videos and read stuff.

> **[NODE: TL + PE confirm]** "LONG TIME" branch of the script. Don't say "props", say "how come?".

**Ștefan:** Seven months on demo, how come you haven't made the jump?

**Daniel:** (pause) I guess I'm scared of losing money. And everything online says something different. I don't know who to trust. Is this actually legit, like are you guys one of those...?

> **CURVEBALL: "is this legit?"** Don't defend with a speech. Normalise, redirect, keep him talking.

**Ștefan:** Good question, and honestly I'd be asking the same thing, there's a lot of noise out there. That's exactly why we're having this call, I'm not here to sell you anything, I just want to see if it even makes sense for you. Can I ask, what's the thing that scares you most, losing money or not knowing what you're doing?

**Daniel:** Not knowing what I'm doing. I'd lose money *because* I don't know what I'm doing.

> **[NODE: WGW]** Found it. The obstacle is not capital, it's knowing what to do + who to trust.

**Ștefan:** So from what I understand, you're a supervisor working 45 hours, just getting by every month, you want a second income of about 1,500 so you can move out and stop stressing, and what's been stopping you for seven months is not knowing how to do it properly and who to trust, correct?

**Daniel:** Yeah. That's literally it.

> **[GAP shown]**

**Ștefan:** And you told me you want to build this up and get out of that spare room... are you actually committed to making that happen now, or are you still just exploring?

**Daniel:** No, I want to do it. I'm tired of waiting.

**Ștefan:** Great. And if there was a way to actually get you there, would you be ready to invest in yourself if that's what it took?

**Daniel:** Depends how much, honestly.

> **CURVEBALL: price.** Use the script's deferral. Don't quote.

**Ștefan:** Fair. This is not a $50 course, it's a real mentorship, and we have different programs based on where you start, how much you have, how much support you need. I wasn't trained on pricing, because it'd be a disservice quoting the wrong program. With that said, how much would you say you have set aside to actually invest in yourself and get you to that second income?

**Daniel:** I could probably put together a couple thousand without wrecking my month. Maybe a bit more if I have to.

**Ștefan:** Okay, that's honest, good. Here's what I can do: I can connect you with [CLOSER], he's a senior trading mentor. He'll get more in depth about you, see what you want, and if it's a fit he'll walk you through what it looks like. Sounds good?

**Daniel:** Yeah, let's do it.

**Ștefan:** Great, let me see if we still got slots. Are you free tomorrow or Thursday?

**Daniel:** Thursday.

**Ștefan:** Morning or evening works best with your shifts?

**Daniel:** Evening, after 7.

**Ștefan:** 7:30 or 8:00?

**Daniel:** 7:30.

**Ștefan:** Locked in for Thursday 7:30. Best email for the call details? ... And is this number good for a reminder text? ... You're locked in, check your email right now and accept the invite for me, I'll wait.

**Daniel:** Got it, accepted.

**Ștefan:** Perfect. If something comes up just message me and we'll reschedule, yeah? I'll send you a couple of videos that fit your situation, watch them, find a quiet spot, on a computer, and [CLOSER] will break it all down. Cool?

**Daniel:** Cool, thanks man.

**Ștefan:** Take care, brother.

## Improv notes (where it could have gone differently)

- If he'd answered "fear of losing money" immediately, go to EI/CS faster and skip repeated TL questions.
- If he'd said "I have about 300 saved", shift to an honest capital conversation *before* pitching, a beginner with no capital shouldn't be pushed to the closer.
- If he'd gone silent after "is this legit?", offer one concrete, honest line ("we'll talk on the next call, no obligation") and move on.
- If the 7-month answer had been "just a couple weeks", use the script's SHORT TIME branch: "props for taking action quickly" → WGW.
