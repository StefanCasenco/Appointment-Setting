# 07 · Prop Firm Chaser

**Path:** INTENT → PE → EI → WGW → G → TL → CS → GAP → URGENCY → FIN QUAL → PITCH → LOCK → SHOW UP
**Lead:** Ahmed, 30, freelance designer. Passes evaluations, loses funded accounts. Has spent about $1,800 on challenge fees.
**Hidden truth:** He treats evals like a lottery. Hasn't admitted that to anyone.
**Curveballs:** blames the prop firms ("rigged"), then tests whether you'll just agree with him.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Ahmed? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded our futures guide, right?

**Ahmed:** Yep.

**Ștefan:** Cool, 10-15 minutes, to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. Good?

**Ahmed:** Go ahead.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Ahmed:** I'm into prop firms. I keep passing the evals, but I can't keep a funded account.

> **[NODE: INTENT → PE]**

**Ștefan:** Nice, passing evals isn't easy. How many have you passed, and what happens after?

**Ahmed:** Seven? Five funded accounts. I lose them all within the first month or two.

**Ștefan:** Seven passes, five funded, and all lost fast. What do you think happens in those first weeks?

**Ahmed:** The firms change rules or they're rigged. The drawdown is too tight, spreads move, whatever.

> **CURVEBALL: "it's rigged."** Don't agree, don't argue. Stay curious.

**Ștefan:** I've heard that a lot. Can I ask, when the account goes, what does the last week of trading look like? Same behavior as the eval, or different?

**Ahmed:** ...Different, I guess. Once it's funded I try to hit payouts faster. I get greedy.

> **[NODE: PE → EI]** He just told you the real problem himself.

**Ștefan:** That's a real answer, most people don't say that. What's that cost you so far? Money and otherwise.

**Ahmed:** About 1,800 in fees. And it's embarrassing. I told my friends I was funded, they ask about payouts, I've got nothing.

> **[NODE: EI]** Shame + money. Sit with it for a beat.

**Ștefan:** Yeah, I can imagine. So if you take the greed out, what do you think is really in the way?

> **[NODE: WGW]**

**Ahmed:** I don't have a system I trust. I pass evals with luck sometimes, a streak. Then I don't know why it worked.

**Ștefan:** So no repeatable process.

**Ahmed:** Exactly.

**Ștefan:** What do you want out of this? What's the goal?

> **[NODE: G]**

**Ahmed:** Hit payouts consistently. Like 4-5k a month across a couple accounts.

**Ștefan:** What would that let you do?

**Ahmed:** I'd drop the design clients I hate. Work on my own schedule.

**Ștefan:** When do you want to be there by?

> **[NODE: TL]**

**Ahmed:** Six months. A year max.

**Ștefan:** What's your day look like with design right now?

> **[NODE: CS]**

**Ahmed:** Freelance, inconsistent income, 2-3k some months, 800 others. I trade in between. It's not stable.

**Ștefan:** So from what I understand, you're a freelancer with unstable income, you've passed seven evals and kept none of the funded accounts because you don't have a repeatable system and you get greedy, you want 4-5k a month in payouts within about a year so you can drop the clients, correct?

**Ahmed:** Yeah. That's honest.

**Ștefan:** Do you want to change this now, or do you want to take one more shot on your own first?

**Ahmed:** I've done that seven times. I'm tired of burning money.

**Ștefan:** Makes sense. If there was a way to get that system and someone to hold you to it, would you be ready to invest in yourself, if that's what it took?

**Ahmed:** Not a problem if it works. But I don't want to pay for another course that tells me nothing.

**Ștefan:** I get that. Not a $50 course, it's a real mentorship, different programs depending on where people are. I wasn't trained on pricing because I'd hate to quote you the wrong one. How much have you set aside?

**Ahmed:** About 3,000.

**Ștefan:** Great. I'll connect you with [CLOSER], he'll go deeper into your situation, and if it's a fit he'll walk you through it. Sounds good?

**Ahmed:** Alright.

**Ștefan:** Are you free tomorrow or Wednesday?

**Ahmed:** Wednesday.

**Ștefan:** Morning or evening?

**Ahmed:** Morning's better. 11?

**Ștefan:** Locked in Wednesday at 11. Best email?... Number for a reminder?... Accept for me now.

**Ahmed:** Done.

**Ștefan:** If something comes up message me. I'll send a couple of videos, watch them, quiet place, computer.
