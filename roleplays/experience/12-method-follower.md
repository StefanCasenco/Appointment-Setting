# 12 · Method Follower (SMC / ICT)

**Path:** INTENT → PE → WGW → G → TL → CS → GAP → URGENCY → FIN QUAL → PITCH → LOCK → SHOW UP
**Lead:** Dylan, 22, college student with part-time work. Deep into ICT/SMC concepts, can mark up a chart beautifully, loses money live.
**Hidden truth:** He knows the vocabulary but has no rules for when to take a trade. He's proud of his knowledge, so he's sensitive about being told he's wrong.
**Curveballs:** jargon overload, and he tries to correct *you*.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Dylan? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded our futures guide, right?

**Dylan:** Yep.

**Ștefan:** Cool, 10-15 minutes, to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. Good?

**Dylan:** Sure.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Dylan:** I've been studying ICT and SMC for about a year. I know all the concepts, fair value gaps, order blocks, liquidity sweeps, but it doesn't click when I trade live.

> **[NODE: INTENT → PE]** "Doesn't click" = trigger for PE + WGW.

**Ștefan:** I don't know all that stuff in depth, that's [CLOSER]'s department. What I'd like to understand is the difference between when you study and when you trade. Are you trading live?

**Dylan:** Yeah, small account. Down a few hundred.

> **CURVEBALL: he corrects you.** If you misuse a term, he'll pounce. Don't bluff. Say "that's not my area."

**Dylan:** It's not "stuff", by the way, it's a whole framework. If you understood price delivery you'd get it.

**Ștefan:** Fair, I'll leave the technical side to the pros, I just want to understand what's going on for you. When you look at the losses, what do you think is going wrong?

> Own the gap, don't compete with his knowledge. Refocus on his experience.

**Dylan:** I see the setups on the chart *after* they happen. In real time, I second-guess. Sometimes I take trades that don't fit.

**Ștefan:** So the concepts are there, the decision in the moment isn't?

**Dylan:** Yeah. And I've got no one to check me. Videos don't tell me why *my* trade was wrong.

> **[NODE: WGW]** Real obstacle = no feedback on his own trades.

**Ștefan:** Makes sense. What do you want out of it, if you get it right?

> **[NODE: G]**

**Dylan:** Funded accounts. Make a living off it after I graduate.

**Ștefan:** What's a living for you? How much?

**Dylan:** 4-5k a month. That's better than most entry-level jobs I'm looking at.

**Ștefan:** When do you graduate?

> **[NODE: TL]**

**Dylan:** Next spring. So like 8 months.

**Ștefan:** What's your situation at the moment? Studies, work?

> **[NODE: CS]**

**Dylan:** Full-time student, part-time job at a coffee shop, about 15 hours. I trade the sessions around that.

**Ștefan:** So from what I understand, you're a student working part-time, you've studied the concepts for a year and trade live with a small account that's down a bit, you want funded accounts and 4-5k a month by graduation, and what's in the way is you don't have anyone checking your actual trades to tell you what's wrong in real time, correct?

**Dylan:** Pretty much, yeah.

**Ștefan:** And do you actually want to change that now, or are you fine learning on your own another year?

**Dylan:** I want to speed it up. I don't want to waste a year.

**Ștefan:** If there was a way to get that feedback, would you be ready to invest in yourself, if that's what it took?

**Dylan:** I mean, I'm a student. It depends how much.

> **Flag.** Student budget. Be honest.

**Ștefan:** I hear you. It's a real mentorship, different programs, I wasn't trained on pricing. Just so I'm not wasting your time, how much would you say you've got set aside, from savings or other, that's *yours* to put toward this?

**Dylan:** Maybe 1,500? I've been saving from the coffee shop.

**Ștefan:** Okay, that's honest. I'll connect you with [CLOSER], he'll go deeper, and he'll tell you honestly if there's a fit. Sounds good?

**Dylan:** Yeah, sure.

**Ștefan:** Free tomorrow or Wednesday?

**Dylan:** Wednesday after class.

**Ștefan:** 4 or 5?

**Dylan:** 4:30.

**Ștefan:** Locked in Wednesday at 4:30. Best email?... Number?... Accept it now.

**Dylan:** Done.

**Ștefan:** I'll send you a couple of videos, quiet place, computer.
