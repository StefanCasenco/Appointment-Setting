# 08 · Pro Scaler

**Path:** INTENT → PE → G → WGW → TL → CS → GAP → URGENCY → FIN QUAL → PITCH → LOCK → SHOW UP
**Lead:** Victor, 36, full-time trader for 3 years. Consistently profitable on a mid-size personal account. Wants to scale into larger funded capital.
**Hidden truth:** He's plateaued. His edge works but he doesn't know how to scale risk without breaking it. He's sceptical of "mentors" and tests you.
**Curveballs:** he opens by challenging you ("what's your track record?") and tries to run the call.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Victor? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded our futures guide, right?

**Victor:** Yeah. Quick question first, who am I talking to? What's your trading background?

> **CURVEBALL: he challenges you.** Don't fake it. Don't get defensive. Stay honest and short.

**Ștefan:** Fair question. I'm not the trader, I'm the one who talks to people first to see if it even makes sense to connect them with the mentors. I'll keep it short, 10-15 minutes, to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. Is that fine?

**Victor:** Fine.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Victor:** I'm already doing it full-time. Three years. I'm consistently profitable, I want to scale, get access to more capital.

> **[NODE: INTENT → PE]** Skip the beginner warm-up. Short call, numbers fast.

**Ștefan:** Three years full-time, that's serious. Can you give me a rough idea of how it looks, what you trade, what you've been making?

**Victor:** Nasdaq futures. I average about 6-8k a month on my own capital. Some funded accounts as well.

**Ștefan:** Solid. What do you want to scale to?

> **[NODE: G]**

**Victor:** 20k+ a month. I want to get to the point where I'm managing real size and not worrying about the account.

**Ștefan:** What changes at 20k that doesn't exist at 7?

**Victor:** Freedom of risk. Right now I'm capped by my account.

**Ștefan:** So what do you think is the thing in the way of scaling to that point?

> **[NODE: WGW]**

**Victor:** The edge works, but when I add size my execution gets sloppy. Slippage, emotional spikes. I haven't solved it.

**Ștefan:** So the edge is fine, it's the scaling process?

**Victor:** Yeah.

**Ștefan:** Have you tried solving it yourself?

**Victor:** Of course. Reduced size, split entries, journaling. Nothing sticks.

**Ștefan:** How long's that been going on?

> **[NODE: TL]**

**Victor:** About eight months. I hit the wall around then.

**Ștefan:** And outside of trading, what's your setup, are you full-time on this or anything else on the side?

> **[NODE: CS]** Short, he's already told you most of it.

**Victor:** Just trading. I quit my job three years ago.

**Ștefan:** Okay. So from what I understand, you're a full-time trader averaging 6-8k a month, you want to scale to 20k+, and what's stopping you is execution at larger size, which you haven't been able to solve alone, correct?

**Victor:** Correct.

**Ștefan:** And do you actually want to fix this now?

**Victor:** If someone can actually show me something I don't already know.

> **CURVEBALL: ego test.** Don't promise. Let the closer prove it.

**Ștefan:** That's the right bar. I'm not going to pretend to know more than you about the markets, what I can do is connect you with [CLOSER], he'll look at your numbers and tell you honestly if there's something there. If not, you won't have lost anything. Fair?

**Victor:** Fair.

**Ștefan:** If there was a way to solve the scaling problem, would you be ready to invest in yourself if that's what it took?

**Victor:** If it's relevant, yeah. Money isn't the issue.

**Ștefan:** It's a real mentorship, different programs depending on where people are. I wasn't trained on pricing, and I'd rather not quote the wrong one. How much have you set aside for investing in this kind of thing?

**Victor:** I'd put 10k toward the right thing.

**Ștefan:** Great. I'll connect you with [CLOSER]. Are you free tomorrow or Thursday?

**Victor:** Thursday morning.

**Ștefan:** 9 or 10?

**Victor:** 10.

**Ștefan:** Locked in Thursday at 10. Best email?... Number?... Accept it now for me.

**Victor:** Done.

**Ștefan:** If something comes up, message me. I'll send a couple of videos, quiet place, computer.

## Improv notes

- If he'd refused to share numbers, say: "No problem, I'm not trying to audit you, I just need to know if it makes sense for [CLOSER] to take the call." Respect protects the booking.
- If he'd been vague on income, the closer will need numbers, so ask once more then flag it.
- Keep this call shorter than the others. A pro doesn't want 12 questions.
