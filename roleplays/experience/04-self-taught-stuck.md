# 04 · Self-Taught, Stuck

**Path:** INTENT → PE → G → TL → WGW → CS → GAP → URGENCY → FIN QUAL → PITCH → LOCK → SHOW UP
**Lead:** Kareem, 34, warehouse. Trading 14 months, self-taught from YouTube. "Can't do it myself." Mostly breaks even.
**Hidden truth:** He's tried three different strategies and blames himself. Sunday is his only free day, relationships suffer.
**Curveballs:** he asks "so are you the one who teaches?", and mid-call a loaded line about his relationship.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Kareem? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded our futures guide, right?

**Kareem:** Yeah, I did.

**Ștefan:** Cool, I've got 10-15 minutes here, just to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. Sounds good?

**Kareem:** Sounds good.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Kareem:** I've actually been trading for a while, but I can't really figure it out by myself. Been watching videos, but I keep running in circles.

> **[NODE: INTENT → PE]** "Can't figure it out myself" = the script's trigger for PAST EXPERIENCE, go right now.

**Ștefan:** How long's a while?

**Kareem:** Fourteen months.

**Ștefan:** Fourteen months, okay. What have you been trading, and how's it been going?

**Kareem:** Futures, ES and NQ mostly. I've tried like three strategies. First an indicator one, then supply and demand, now I'm doing ICT stuff. I'm basically break even. Some weeks I'm up a bit, some weeks I give it back.

**Ștefan:** So you're not blowing accounts, but you're not growing either?

**Kareem:** Yeah. Exactly.

> **[NODE: PE complete]** Break-even trader. Next: G.

**Ștefan:** If it worked, what would you want out of this? What's the number that'd make it worth your time?

**Kareem:** I'd say 6-7k a month. That'd let me quit the job.

**Ștefan:** Quit the job completely, or on the side?

**Kareem:** Quit. Fully.

> **[NODE: G]** Not "more money", quitting the job. Real goal. Don't yet ask about CS, the order says TL next.

**Ștefan:** When are you hoping to have that by?

**Kareem:** Honestly, I should've been there already. Maybe within the year.

**Ștefan:** A year. Okay. You've been at it for 14 months, what do you feel got in the way of that so far?

> **[NODE: TL → WGW]**

**Kareem:** I think I don't have anyone who actually knows. I'm piecing it together from videos and half of them contradict each other. I don't have someone to tell me "this is wrong". So I just try something else.

**Ștefan:** So the missing piece is someone who's done it showing you what's right and checking your work?

**Kareem:** Yeah, that.

> Optional "teach back": say it in your words, he agrees, now he's said the need himself.

**Ștefan:** Makes sense. What do you do right now for work?

> **[NODE: CS]** Last, per the order.

**Kareem:** Warehouse. I work Tuesday to Sunday, around 60 hours.

**Ștefan:** 60 hours, Tuesday to Sunday. So you've got one day off?

**Kareem:** Just Monday. And I spend it trading or sleeping.

**Ștefan:** What does that do to everything else? Relationships, friends...

> **[NODE: EI, loaded]** Don't rush. This is the "dog died" moment, stay in it.

**Kareem:** (exhales) Not good. My girlfriend says I'm never around. We're kind of on the edge, honestly.

**Ștefan:** I'm sorry man, that's heavy. Is that part of why quitting the job matters so much?

**Kareem:** That's the *whole* reason. I don't care about the money itself, I want my time back.

> Real WHY = time/relationship. Use it in the gap.

**Ștefan:** I get it. So from what I understand, you work 60 hours, six days, you've been trading 14 months and you're break-even, you want to get to about 6-7k a month so you can quit and save the relationship, and what's holding you back is you've been doing it alone without someone showing you what's right, correct?

**Kareem:** Yes. Word for word.

**Ștefan:** And you want this to change now?

**Kareem:** I don't have another year to waste.

**Ștefan:** Got it. If there was a way to actually get you there, would you be ready to invest in yourself if that's what it took?

**Kareem:** If it works, yeah.

**Ștefan:** Not a $50 course, it's a real mentorship. Different programs depending on where people are. How much would you say you've set aside to invest in getting to quit the job?

**Kareem:** Maybe 3,000, 4,000? I can stretch.

> **CURVEBALL:** "So are you the one who teaches this?"

**Kareem:** Wait, are you the one who teaches this?

**Ștefan:** No man, I'm the one who gets to know you first. [CLOSER] is a senior mentor, he'll go deeper on your situation and if it's a fit he'll show you what it looks like. Sounds good?

**Kareem:** Yeah, that works.

**Ștefan:** Are you free Monday, since that's your day off, or Tuesday morning?

**Kareem:** Monday.

**Ștefan:** Morning or afternoon?

**Kareem:** Afternoon, 2?

**Ștefan:** Locked in Monday at 2. Best email?... Number for a reminder?... Check it and accept for me.

**Kareem:** Got it.

**Ștefan:** If something comes up, message me. I'll send you a couple of videos, watch them, quiet place, computer. [CLOSER] will break it all down.

**Kareem:** Alright, thanks.
