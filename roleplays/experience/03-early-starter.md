# 03 · Early Starter

**Path:** INTENT → PE → WGW → G → TL → CS → GAP → URGENCY → FIN QUAL → PITCH → LOCK → SHOW UP
**Lead:** Tomás, 31, delivery driver. Four months in, small live account, down about $400. Follows a Discord group.
**Hidden truth:** He's emotionally attached to the account size and ashamed of the losses. He doesn't want to tell his girlfriend how much he's lost.
**Curveballs:** says "I'm doing fine, just need tweaks" (ego mask), then jumps to "how much is it".

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Tomás? It's Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded the futures guide, that right?

**Tomás:** Yeah that's me.

**Ștefan:** Cool, I got 10-15 minutes, just to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. Good?

**Tomás:** Yeah, go ahead.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Tomás:** Started a few months ago. I'm actually trading already, just looking to tighten things up.

> **[NODE: INTENT → PE]** "Already trading" = go to PE now. Keep it casual so he doesn't get defensive.

**Ștefan:** Nice, taking action, that's the hardest part. How long's a few months?

**Tomás:** Like four. I opened a small account, trading micros on the Nasdaq.

**Ștefan:** And how's it going so far?

**Tomás:** It's okay. Up and down. Nothing crazy.

> Soft answer. Don't challenge. Ask for a specific.

**Ștefan:** Up and down how, like overall are you green or red right now?

**Tomás:** ...A bit red. Like 400 down. But it's just the learning curve.

> **[NODE: EI, early, softly]** The shame is right there. Respond human, don't pounce.

**Ștefan:** Yeah, honestly almost everyone starts there, and you're being straight about it, appreciate that. When you look back at the trades that went wrong, what do you feel actually got in the way?

> **[NODE: WGW]** Per his order, take it right after PE.

**Tomás:** I don't know... I get into trades too early. Or I move my stop. Or I follow what the Discord says and it doesn't work. I don't really have a plan, it's whatever feels right that day.

**Ștefan:** So kind of trading on gut, with different people telling you different things?

**Tomás:** Yeah. Pretty much.

**Ștefan:** What would it look like for you if you got that fixed? How much would you want to be making?

**Tomás:** I just want to be steady. Like a thousand a month, to start. Then more.

> **[NODE: G]** "Steady" = vague but he gave a number. Fine.

**Ștefan:** What would a thousand a month change for you right now?

**Tomás:** I wouldn't have to do 12-hour days in the van. And honestly my girlfriend and I want to get a place, it's hard right now.

**Ștefan:** 12-hour days, how many days a week?

**Tomás:** Six. Sometimes seven.

> **[NODE: CS + EI]** The van schedule is the cost of the plan not working.

**Ștefan:** That's brutal. How long have you been doing that?

**Tomás:** Three years.

**Ștefan:** And you started trading four months ago, so is this the fix you've been looking for?

**Tomás:** Pretty much. I just can't mess it up.

> **CURVEBALL: price too early.**

**Tomás:** What's the price on your program anyway?

**Ștefan:** Yeah, I wasn't trained on pricing, there are different programs, and quoting the wrong one wouldn't do you any favors. Let me make sure I get the picture first. So you're driving six days, 12-hour days, four months into trading, about 400 down, you want to get to a steady 1,000 a month so you can cut the hours and get a place with your girlfriend, and what's holding you back is you've got no clear plan, you just follow gut and Discord, correct?

**Tomás:** Yeah. That's about right.

**Ștefan:** And you want this changed now, right? Or is it a "someday" thing?

**Tomás:** Now. I can't lose more money. That's why I figured maybe I should learn properly.

**Ștefan:** That makes sense. If there was a way to get you a real plan and someone to check your work, would you be ready to invest in yourself, if that's what it took?

**Tomás:** Yeah, if it's not insane.

**Ștefan:** I hear you. It's a real mentorship, different programs, so I'd want [CLOSER] to see where you fit. How much would you say you've put aside for this, on top of your trading account?

**Tomás:** Not a ton. Maybe 1,500-2,000 if I tighten everything.

> Honest but tight. Note it for the closer ("budget-sensitive").

**Ștefan:** Okay, honest answer, I appreciate it. Here's what I can do, I'll connect you with [CLOSER], he'll go deeper into you, and if it's a fit he'll show you what that looks like. Sounds good?

**Tomás:** Yeah.

**Ștefan:** Are you free tomorrow or Wednesday?

**Tomás:** Wednesday, but late, after 9.

**Ștefan:** 9 or 9:30?

**Tomás:** 9:30.

**Ștefan:** Locked in, Wednesday 9:30. Best email?... Number for a reminder text?... Check your email and accept for me.

**Tomás:** Done.

**Ștefan:** If something comes up, message me. I'll send you a couple of videos, watch them, find a quiet place, on a computer, [CLOSER] will break it down.

**Tomás:** Alright, cool.
