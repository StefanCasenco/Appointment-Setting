# 13 · Already Has a Mentor

**Path:** INTENT → PE → WGW → G → EI → TL → CS → GAP → URGENCY → FIN QUAL → PITCH → LOCK → SHOW UP
**Lead:** Nate, 40, project manager. Currently in a paid mentorship (about 5 months in), not seeing results. Comparing options.
**Hidden truth:** He feels he's already sunk $3k and doesn't want to "double pay". He's not sure whether to blame the program or himself.
**Curveballs:** "I'm already in a program, why am I talking to you?", and "I'll think about it" at the end.

---

## The call

**Ștefan:** Heeey Nate? Ștefan from [COMPANY], looks like you downloaded our futures guide, right?

**Nate:** Yeah. Just so you know, I'm already in a program.

> **CURVEBALL: "why am I here?"** Don't pitch against the other program. Get curious.

**Ștefan:** Appreciate you saying that. 10-15 minutes, to understand where you're at, where you wanna be, and if I can help I'll point you in the right direction. If you're happy where you are, I'll tell you so. Fair?

**Nate:** Sure.

**Ștefan:** What had you interested in trading in the first place?

**Nate:** Wanted a second income. I'm in a mentorship, about five months in. Honestly, I'm not seeing what I expected.

> **[NODE: INTENT → PE]**

**Ștefan:** How come? What's been missing?

> **[NODE: WGW]** Straight to the issue.

**Nate:** There's group calls, a Discord, some videos. But nobody looks at my trades. I ask a question, I get a generic answer. I feel like a number.

**Ștefan:** That's frustrating, you're paying for guidance and it feels generic. How long have you been trading overall?

**Nate:** A year and a bit. Five months with them.

**Ștefan:** How are your results?

**Nate:** Break-even. Maybe slightly down.

**Ștefan:** What did you hope the program would do for you?

> **[NODE: G]**

**Nate:** I wanted 3-4k a month. And honestly, I wanted to cut back at work. I'm a project manager, 50-hour weeks, tons of stress.

**Ștefan:** Cut back or quit?

**Nate:** Go part-time first. Eventually leave.

**Ștefan:** What's that stress doing to you, day-to-day?

> **[NODE: EI]**

**Nate:** I'm tired. I've got two kids, I'm missing stuff. My daughter's recitals, that kind of thing. I want my evenings back.

**Ștefan:** I hear you. That's a big reason. How long are you giving yourself to get there?

> **[NODE: TL]**

**Nate:** A year, maybe less. I can't keep doing this.

**Ștefan:** And outside of trading, what's your day look like, how much of it is work?

> **[NODE: CS]**

**Nate:** 50 hours at work, commute on top. I trade early mornings, a couple of hours.

**Ștefan:** So from what I understand, you're a PM working 50 hours with two kids, you've been trading about a year, you're five months into a program that doesn't look at your trades, you want 3-4k a month so you can go part-time and be there for your kids, and what's in the way is you're not getting personal, real feedback, correct?

**Nate:** Yes. Exactly that.

**Ștefan:** Do you want to change that now, or are you giving the current program more time?

**Nate:** I've given it five months. I need something different.

> **CURVEBALL: "I'm already invested."**

**Nate:** I just don't want to pay twice, you know? I've already put in 3,000.

**Ștefan:** I get that, and I'm not going to tell you to just walk away from what you've paid. But what's the cost of staying where you are another six months without results? Is that cheaper?

> Frame cost-of-inaction, not a hard-sell. Don't rubbish the other program.

**Nate:** ...No. Probably not.

**Ștefan:** If there was a way to get the personal feedback you're missing, would you be ready to invest in yourself, if it was the right fit?

**Nate:** Yeah, if it's actually different.

**Ștefan:** It's a real mentorship, different programs, I wasn't trained on pricing. How much have you got set aside for something like this?

**Nate:** I could stretch to 3,500. Not more.

**Ștefan:** Okay. I'll connect you with [CLOSER], he'll go deeper, you can ask exactly how it's different. Sounds good?

**Nate:** Alright.

**Ștefan:** Free tomorrow or Thursday?

**Nate:** Thursday evening, after the kids are in bed.

**Ștefan:** 8:30 or 9?

**Nate:** 9.

**Ștefan:** Locked in Thursday at 9. Best email?... Number?... Accept it now.

**Nate:** Done.

**Ștefan:** If something comes up, message me. I'll send some videos, quiet place, computer.

## Improv notes

- If he'd said "my mentor is great, just checking options", get what he *wants more of* instead. If nothing, the lead is cold, thank him and leave the door open.
- If he'd said "I'm locked in a contract for a year", ask how that's working for him. Real obstacle might be the contract, which is not your problem to solve.
- Never badmouth the other mentorship. "I can't speak to them" is plenty.
